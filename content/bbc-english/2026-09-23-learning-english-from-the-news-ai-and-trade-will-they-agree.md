## 新闻故事摘要

### The Story

**US President Donald Trump and China's President Xi Jinping will meet in a summit at the White House this week for the first time since 2023**, following on from President Trump's visit to China earlier this year in which a truce was agreed.

**美国总统特朗普与中国国家主席习近平本周将在白宫举行峰会**，这是 2023 年以来首次会晤，此前特朗普今年早些时候访华已达成休战协议。

**Before the truce was agreed, both the US and China had imposed high tariffs on products coming in from the other country.** The truce will end in November and many experts say that the two countries will try to extend their truce and make more agreements on trade.

**休战前，美中双方均对彼此商品征收高额关税。** 休战将于 11 月到期，专家预测双方将寻求延长休战并达成更多贸易协议。

**Alongside trade tariffs, there are other potential disagreements between the two leaders.** For example over AI and the war in Iran. There are talks about a warning system for AI that Trump plans to share with Xi.

**除贸易关税外，两国领导人在 AI 与伊朗问题上也存在分歧。** 正在讨论特朗普计划与习近平共享的 AI 预警系统。

## 详细词汇解析

### 1. **loom large** (动词短语)

> **定义**：*be a very important, often worrying issue*
> 
> **中文**：显得突出；迫在眉睫；构成重大威胁/隐患

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **新闻标题** | *AI, tariffs and Iran **loom large**.* | AI、关税、伊朗问题**构成重大隐患/摆在首位**。 |
| **社会议题** | *The impact of social media on kids **looms large** for many parents.* | 社交媒体对孩子的影响**让许多家长深感忧虑/悬而未决**。 |
| **经济风险** | *Inflation **looms large** over the economy.* | 通胀**笼罩/威胁**着经济。 |

**同义表达**：*hang over, cast a shadow over, dominate, be a major concern*

---

### 2. **time is on someone's side** (习语)

> **定义**：*someone has lots of time in which to do a particular thing, they don't have to act quickly*
> 
> **中文**：时间站在某人这边；某人有充裕时间，不必急于行动

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **外交博弈** | *With **time on his side**, Xi seeks to extend the truce.* | **时间站在习近平这边**，他寻求延长休战。 |
| **求职准备** | *I found out my company would be hiring soon, so **time was on my side** to work on my CV.* | **我有充裕时间**准备简历。 |
| **投资策略** | *Young investors have **time on their side** for compound interest.* | 年轻投资者**时间优势**明显，利于复利。 |

**反义表达**：*time is running out, pressed for time, against the clock*

---

### 3. **hotline** (名词)

> **定义**：*a special, direct phone connection often used in emergencies*
> 
> **中文**：热线；直通电话；专线；红色电话

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **外交/军事** | *Trump plans to pitch an AI **hotline** to Xi.* | 特朗普拟向习近平提议建立 AI **热线/直通机制**。 |
| **客服/举报** | *The company was criticised for replacing its complaints **hotline** with an online form.* | 公司用在线表单替换投诉 **热线** 引发批评。 |
| **心理援助** | *Suicide prevention **hotline** operates 24/7.* | 防自杀 **热线** 24 小时运作。 |

**常见搭配**：
- *establish/set up a hotline* (建立热线)
- *direct hotline* (直通热线)
- *emergency hotline* (紧急热线)
- *AI hotline / nuclear hotline* (AI/核热线)

## 批判性思考题

1. **AI 治理的 "Hotline" 机制**：*AI hotline* 类比 *nuclear hotline* (红色电话)，能否有效防范 *AI arms race* (AI 军备竞赛) 与 *misalignment accidents* (目标错位事故)？需解决 *attribution problem* (归因难题) 与 *verification* (核查机制)。

2. **关税休战的博弈论**：*truce extension* 属于 *"repeated game"* (重复博弈) 中的 *"cooperative equilibrium"* (合作均衡)。若 *time is on Xi's side*，美方谈判筹码如何变化？*asymmetric interdependence* (不对称相互依存) 如何影响让步？

3. **"Loom large" 的修辞力量**：标题用 *loom large* 而非 *are major issues*，如何通过 *"spatial metaphor"* (空间隐喻) 强化 *"salience & threat"* (显著性与威胁感)？媒体标题常用 *"loom, specter, shadow"* 词族。

4. **中美 AI 合作的信任赤字**：*verification, transparency, data sharing* 三大支柱缺一不可。*hotline* 仅为 *"confidence-building measure" (建立信任措施)*，能否推动 *"norm-setting" (规范制定)*？

5. **新闻英语的标题词汇学**：*loom large, seek stability, pitch a hotline, on his side* — 标题如何在有限字数内编码 *"agency, urgency, strategic framing"*？对比 CNBC / NYT / Al Jazeera 的立场差异。
