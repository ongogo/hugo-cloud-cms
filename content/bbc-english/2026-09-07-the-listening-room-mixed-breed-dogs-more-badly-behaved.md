## 研究详细内容

### 研究设计
- **机构**: Royal Veterinary College (皇家兽医学院)
- **样本**: 9,000+ 只狗的主人问卷（英国）
- **对比组**: Doodle dogs (贵宾犬杂交) vs 亲本纯种品种
- **热门品种**: Cockapoos, Labradoodles, Cavapoos

### 关键发现
| 发现 | 详情 |
|------|------|
| **行为更差比例** | 有差异时，8/10 情况下 doodle 行为更 undesirable |
| **最常见问题** | 1. Fear of loud noises (怕噪音)<br>2. Separation anxiety (分离焦虑)<br>3. Training problems (训练困难)<br>4. Aggression (攻击性) |
| **训练/焦虑/攻击** | 特别在训练、焦虑、攻击三方面表现超出预期 |

### 专家观点
> **Royal Veterinary College**: *"Doodle dogs, bred from a poodle crossed with a different breed, have grown in popularity in recent years, but they don't always behave as expected, particularly around training, anxiety and aggression."*
> 
> **皇家兽医学院**: *"混种贵宾犬近年来大受欢迎，但表现不总如预期，尤其在训练、焦虑、攻击方面。"*

## 批判性思考题

1. **杂交优势 vs 行为风险**：*hybrid vigor* (杂交优势) 通常指健康/体质，但行为层面可能出现 *behavioral mismatch*。如何用英语讨论 *"genetic unpredictability in crossbreeds"*？

2. **育种伦理与消费者保护**：*designer dogs* (设计师犬) 高价销售，买家往往不知行为风险。相关英语：*puppy farming, backyard breeders, health testing, temperament screening, consumer awareness*。

3. **行为问题的成因**：*genetics vs environment* (先天 vs 后天)。混种行为差是基因重组、早期社会化缺失、还是主人预期管理不当？(*nature vs nurture, critical socialization period*)

4. **兽医行为学术语**：*undesirable behaviours, aggression, anxiety, fear response, noise phobia, separation-related behaviours, trainability*。如何建立标准化行为评估量表？

5. **流行趋势的代价**：社交媒体推波助澜 *doodle craze*。如何用英语表达 *"trend-driven breeding", "impulse purchase", "surrender to shelters"*？

The most common issues were **fear of loud noises**, **separation anxiety** and problems with training.
