### 中文简介

本期节目介绍实用表达 **"keep yourself to yourself"**（独来独往；保持低调；不爱社交）。意思是**某人不怎么与人交往、喜欢独处、不分享私生活**，通常指性格内向或私密的人。这是**中性描述**，不含贬义——既不代表不友善，也不代表神秘，只是偏好独处。Becca 和 Feifei 通过办公室邻居、旅行、疲惫等场景展示用法，并解释了代词变化和 "keep something to yourself"(守秘密) 的区别。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达用法

> **"He really seems to keep himself to himself."**
> 
> 他看起来真的**很独来独往 / 很低调**。

**关键点**：
- ✅ 固定短语，中性色彩（不褒不贬）
- ✅ **代词随主语变化**：*keep myself/yourself/himself/herself/ourselves/themselves to myself/yourself/himself/herself/ourselves/themselves*
- ✅ **两个不同含义**：
  1. **人** *keep oneself to oneself* = 独来独往、不爱社交
  2. **事** *keep something to yourself* = 守秘密、不透露

### 2. 语法重点：反身代词变化

| 主语 | 反身代词 | 完整形式 |
|------|----------|----------|
| I | myself | **keep myself to myself** |
| You | yourself | **keep yourself to yourself** |
| He/She | himself/herself | **keep himself/herself to himself/herself** |
| We | ourselves | **keep ourselves to ourselves** |
| They | themselves | **keep themselves to themselves** |

### 3. 同义/相关表达对比

| 表达 | 含义 | 色彩 |
|------|------|------|
| **keep yourself to yourself** | 独来独往、低调 | 中性 |
| **keep to oneself** | 同义，略简短 | 中性 |
| **be a loner** | 独行者、孤僻者 | 略贬义 |
| **be introverted** | 内向 | 描述性格，中性 |
| **be private / value privacy** | 重隐私 | 中性偏正面 |
| **be standoffish** | 冷淡疏远 | 贬义 |
| **keep something to yourself** | 守秘密 | 语境依赖 |

## 课文实例解析

### Example 1: 疲惫想独处
> *"I'm just going to **keep myself to myself** today. I'm so tired."*
> 
> 今天我就**想独自待着 / 不想理人**。我太累了。

### Example 2: 邻居不爱社交
> *"I've been trying to get to know my new neighbour recently, but I think she likes to **keep herself to herself**."*
> 
> 我最近想跟新邻居熟悉一下，但我觉得她**喜欢独来独往 / 不爱跟人打交道**。

### Example 3: 旅行风格差异
> *"When I go travelling on my own, I usually want to meet up with people, but there are others who just want to **keep themselves to themselves**."*
> 
> 我独自旅行时通常想认识新朋友，但也有人**只想独自待着 / 不想社交**。

### Example 4: 代词变化 + 守秘密对比
> **Becca**: *"You can change the pronoun... She could **keep herself to herself**, or maybe there's a lot going on and I want to **keep myself to myself**. And with a very different meaning, if we say that we **keep something to ourselves**, it means we're keeping a secret."*
> 
> **Becca**: *"代词可以变... 她可以**独来独往**，或者我有事想**独自待着**。完全不同的意思是，如果我们说**keep something to ourselves**，意思是**守秘密**。"*

## 批判性思考题

1. **文化差异**：英语文化中 "keep yourself to yourself" 是中性/可接受的，但在某些集体主义文化中可能被视为"不合群""冷漠"。如何跨文化解释这种性格差异？

2. **内向 vs 社交焦虑**：*keep yourself to yourself* 描述的是选择性独处，但外人可能误读为社交恐惧。如何用英语区分 *introversion, social anxiety, shyness, aloofness*？

3. **代词语法**：为什么英语要求 *keep oneself to oneself* 双重反身代词？这种结构在英语中常见吗？(*pride oneself on, avail oneself of, perjure oneself* 等)

4. **双关趣味**：Feifei 结尾 *"glad you didn't keep this expression to yourself"* 玩了什么文字游戏？双关双义 *keep to yourself* (不分享/独处) 的修辞效果？

5. **现代职场**：开放式办公室、团队建设、社交媒体曝光的今天，"keeping yourself to yourself" 是否越来越难？如何用英语表达"需要独处时间充电"而不冒犯同事？

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Feifei**: Hello and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain phrases and expressions used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them too. I'm Feifei.

**Becca**: And I'm Becca.

**Feifei**: Becca, our new office neighbour seems nice, but I hardly ever see him talking to anyone.

**Becca**: Yes, he really seems to **keep himself to himself**.

**Feifei**: Hmm, what does that mean?

**Becca**: If someone **keeps themself to themselves**, they don't spend much time socialising with other people. They prefer to be on their own and usually don't share much about their personal life. Someone who is **introverted** could prefer to **keep themself to themselves**.

**Feifei**: So, does it mean they're unfriendly?

**Becca**: Not at all. Someone who **keeps themself to themselves** can be perfectly friendly. It just means they're **private** or quiet. It's usually a **neutral** description, although sometimes people use it to suggest someone is a bit **mysterious**.

**Feifei**: Ah, I see. Now, let's hear some examples.

**Examples**:
> I'm just going to **keep myself to myself** today. I'm so tired.

> I've been trying to get to know my new neighbour recently, but I think she likes to **keep herself to herself**.

> When I go travelling on my own, I usually want to meet up with people, but there are others who just want to **keep themselves to themselves**.

**Feifei**: Good examples there. Becca, can I say, **keep ourselves to ourselves**?

**Becca**: Yes, you certainly can! You can change the pronoun depending on who we're talking about. She could **keep herself to herself**, or maybe there's a lot going on and I want to **keep myself to myself**. And with a very different meaning, if we say that we **keep something to ourselves**, it means we're keeping a secret or just aren't sharing it. I guess it has a similar mysterious feeling as someone who **keeps yourself to yourself**.

**Feifei**: Well, I'm glad you didn't **keep this expression to yourself** and instead decided to share it with us. That's it for this programme. Join us next time for more useful English expressions. Goodbye!

**Becca**: Bye!
