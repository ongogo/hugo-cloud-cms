### 中文简介

本期节目讲解高频词 **'drop'** 的 **4 个不同含义**（除常见的"让落下"外）：
1. **少量液体** — *a drop of rain / chilli oil*
2. **停止做/不再包含** — *drop an activity / drop someone from a team*
3. **开车送某人到某地** — *drop sb (off) at...*
4. **(歌曲/视频等)发布、上线** — *it drops Friday / have just dropped online*

Phil 通过生动例句快速演示每个用法，适合碎片化学习。

## 4 个含义详解

### 1. **small amount of something, usually liquid** — 少量液体

> **用法**：*a drop of + 不可数名词 (通常指液体)*
> 
> **含义**：一小滴、极少量

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *I planted all these seeds, but we haven't had a **drop of rain**.* | 我种下所有种子，但**一滴雨**都没下。 |
| *I only put a **drop of chilli oil** into your ramen – it shouldn't be too hot!* | 我只在你的拉面里放了**一滴辣椒油**——不会太辣的！ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 常用搭配：*a drop of rain/water/oil/perfume/blood*
- ✅ 也可比喻"极少量"：*not a drop of common sense* (毫无常识)
- ✅ 固定短语：*not a drop to drink* (滴水难寻)

---

### 2. **stop doing/stop including** — 停止做/不再包含/除名

> **用法**：*drop + 动名词/名词* / *be dropped from*
> 
> **含义**：放弃某活动、停止某项目、将某人从名单/团队中移除

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *I'm going to **drop playing rugby** – I keep getting injured.* | 我要**放弃打橄榄球**——老受伤。 |
| *Charlie's stopped practising her clarinet and I'm worried she will be **dropped from** wind orchestra.* | Charlie 不练单簧管了，担心她会被**从管弦乐团除名/开除**。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ *drop doing sth* = 放弃/停止做某事
- ✅ *drop a project/course/subject* = 放弃项目/课程/科目
- ✅ *drop sb from the team/list* = 从名单中除名某人（被动常用 *be dropped from*）
- ✅ 语气比 *quit/stop* 更随意、口语化

---

### 3. **take to a place, usually by driving** — 开车送某人到某地

> **用法**：*drop sb (off) at/in...*
> 
> **含义**：顺路/专程开车送某人到某地，然后离开

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Jump in! I can **drop you at** the station.* | 上车！我**送你到**车站。 |
| *Will you **drop me off at** the shops please?* | 能**顺道把我放在**商店那边吗？ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 可加 *off*：*drop sb off* / *drop off sb*（同义，更常用）
- ✅ 隐含"顺路"或"不绕远路"之意
- ✅ 反义：*pick sb up* (接人)
- ✅ 常用场景：*drop kids at school, drop colleague at airport, drop off package*

---

### 4. **become available** — (歌曲/视频/专辑等)发布、上线

> **用法**：*sth drops* / *sth has dropped* (非正式、现代用法)
> 
> **含义**：新作品正式发布、上线、可供获取

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Make sure you catch my new song. It **drops** this Friday.* | 别错过我的新歌，**本周五发布/上线**。 |
| *Ten more episodes of my favourite series have just **dropped online**.* | 我最爱的剧**刚上线了**十集新剧集。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ **非正式、网络/娱乐圈黑话**，近年极度流行
- ✅ 主语通常是 *song, album, video, episode, series, mixtape, collection*
- ✅ 时态：*drops* (定时将来) / *has dropped / just dropped* (刚发布)
- ✅ 同义：*is released, comes out, goes live, lands*

## 词汇总览表

| 含义编号 | 英文标签 | 词性 | 核心结构 | 典型场景 |
|----------|----------|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | small amount of liquid | 名词 | *a drop of + 液体* | 天气、烹饪、医药、比喻 |
| 2 | stop doing/including | 动词 | *drop doing sth / drop sb from* | 运动、教育、工作、项目管理 |
| 3 | give a lift | 动词 | *drop sb (off) at/in* | 通勤、接送、社交 |
| 4 | be released/published | 不及物动词 | *sth drops / has dropped* | 音乐、影视、游戏、时尚发布 |

## 批判性思考题

1. **核心隐喻演变**：*drop* 原义"落下"，如何通过"落下→微小液滴""落下→放弃/停止""落下→放下人/货""落下→新作降临"四条隐喻路径衍生出四大义项？画出语义网络图。

2. **被动 vs 主动**：含义 2 *be dropped from* 常用被动，含义 4 *it drops* 用主动。为什么"被除名"用被动、"专辑发布"用主动？体现了什么施事性/受事性差异？

3. **口语化程度梯度**：四义中哪个最正式？哪个最非正式？(*drop = release* 属网络亚文化黑话，*a drop of* 属标准英语)。如何判断场合得体性？

4. **Phrasal verb 扩展**：*drop off* (送人/下降), *drop by* (顺道拜访), *drop out* (退学/退出), *drop in* (顺道进去), *drop behind* (落后)。这些短语动词如何体现 "down/off/out" 的方向性隐喻？

5. **文化传播**："It drops Friday" 这种用法源于嘻哈/街头文化，现已主流化。类似的"亚文化词汇→大众用语"在中文里有哪些？(*上线/掉线/炸了/上热搜/官宣/塌房*)

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Phil**: What do you know about the word 'drop'? I'm sure you know this meaning... but what about these? 'Drop' can mean a very small amount of something, especially something liquid, like water.

**Example**: *I planted all these seeds, but we haven't had a drop of rain.*

**Phil**: If you drop an activity or a project, you stop doing it. If you drop someone, you stop including them in a group or team.

**Example**: *I'm going to drop playing rugby – I keep getting injured.*

**Phil**: You can also drop someone somewhere, and that means that you take them there, usually in a car.

**Example**: *Jump in! I can drop you at the station.*

**Phil**: This one's quite informal, but if something like a song, a film or a series drops, then it becomes available.

**Example**: *Make sure you catch my new song. It drops this Friday.*

**Phil**: I just dropped four meanings of 'drop' for you. Learn them today.
