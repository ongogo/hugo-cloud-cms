### Part 2: 再听一次，回答选择题（限时1分钟阅读题目）

| 题目 | 选项 | 正确答案 |
|------|------|----------|
| 1. Why did Harry want to do the wing walk? | a. to fulfil a childhood dream / b. to raise money for charity / c. both | **c. both** |
| 2. Harry flew planes during the Second World War. | a. True / b. False / c. Not given | **b. False** |
| 3. How many years after Harry worked in a factory making aircrafts did he perform the wing walk? | a. under 80 / b. 80 / c. over 80 | **c. over 80** |
| 4. What does Harry want people to focus on? | a. the fact that someone his age can do stunts like this / b. the charity he is fundraising for / c. the amount of money he has raised | **b. the charity he is fundraising for** |
| 5. Harry is now thinking about doing a famous race. | a. True / b. False / c. Not given | **a. True** |

**详细答案解析**:
1. **c. both** - 他既想圆童年飞行梦，也想为慈善筹款（Lennox Children's Cancer Fund，已筹近 $7,000）
2. **b. False** - 二战时他在制造登陆日用飞机的工厂工作，并非驾驶飞机
3. **c. over 80** - 青少年时在飞机厂工作（约 1944 年），2026 年翼上行走，相隔 80 多年
4. **b. the charity** - 他坚持重点不应在他身上，而应关注他为之筹款的慈善机构
5. **a. True** - 计划在理疗师帮助下完成伦敦马拉松

## 批判性思考题

1. **高龄挑战极限的社会意义**：98 岁完成翼上行走，如何重塑大众对"老年能力"的认知？如何用英语表达 *"redefining aging" / "challenging ageist stereotypes"*？

2. **慈善与个人挑战的结合**：*"The focus shouldn't be on him"* — 这种利用个人壮举为慈善引流的模式，在英语中常用哪些词汇？(*fundraising stunt, awareness campaign, leverage platform for good*)

3. **二战记忆与航空情怀**：从制造飞机到亲自飞行，跨越 80 年的圆梦。如何用英语表达 *"full circle moment" / "coming full circle" / "lifelong dream realized"*？

4. **老年康复与物理治疗**：从爬不上楼梯到独立登机，理疗师的作用。相关英语词汇：*rehabilitation, mobility training, functional independence, geriatric physiotherapy*。

4. **吉尼斯世界纪录的申请流程**：*adjudicator, attempt, verification, evidence, record title, officiate*。如何用英语描述"打破纪录/创造纪录/保持纪录"？

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Presenter**: A year ago, Harry Heasman couldn't climb the stairs and had to enter an **old age home**. But then, the 98-year-old decided he'd like to **fulfil a childhood dream**.

As a teenager during the Second World War, Harry worked in a factory making aircrafts used in the D-Day landings. He wondered what it **would be like** to fly in one of the planes.

On Saturday, after more than eight decades, Harry stopped wondering. The 98-year-old was **strapped** to the wing of a 1940s **biplane** as it **took to the skies**. In doing so, he broke the Guinness World Record for the oldest person ever to complete a **wing walk**.

In preparation for the flight, Harry rebuilt his strength with a **physiotherapist**. He can now walk up the stairs independently again, a **feat** that **came in handy** for the wing walk because he had to step up to the top of the plane.

But Harry has insisted that the **focus of the stunt shouldn't be on him**. Instead, he wants to **raise awareness** about the charity he is fundraising for – the Lennox Children's Cancer Fund. Harry has raised nearly $7,000 so far.

The 98-year-old plans to continue to work with his physiotherapist, with his **sights now set on** completing the London Marathon.
