## 新闻故事摘要

### The Story

Just weeks after hundreds of grocery stores in France were hit by cyber-attacks, shops in Belgium, Sweden, Ireland, Luxembourg and Estonia have also been attacked.

仅几周前法国数百家杂货店遭遇网络攻击后，比利时、瑞典、爱尔兰、卢森堡和爱沙尼亚的商店也遭到袭击。

The attacks, which increased temperatures in fridges and freezers across Europe, have forced stores to throw away lots of food. In some cases, fridges in stores got to 50C, and some supermarkets in France have experienced fires.

这些攻击使欧洲各地的冰柜和冷藏柜温度升高，迫使商店丢弃大量食物。有些情况下，商店内冰箱温度高达 50℃，法国部分超市还发生了火灾。

Since these attacks on fridges and freezers, people are concerned about how much we use or rely on things that are connected to the internet that could potentially be controlled and hackers.

自这些冰柜和冷藏柜受袭以来，人们开始担心我们使用和依赖的联网设备可能被黑客控制。

## 详细词汇解析

### 1. **on alert** (短语)

> **定义**：watchful, attentive, and prepared
>
> **中文**：警惕的；戒备的；准备就绪的

|| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **安保** | *Security staff were **on alert** after the threat was reported.* | 安保人员在接到威胁报告后保持**警惕**。 |
| **交通** | *Drivers need to be **on alert** for slippery roads in winter.* | 冬季开车时，司机需对湿滑路面保持**警惕**。 |
| **网络安全** | *Companies are **on alert** for new cyber-attacks.* | 公司正对新的网络攻击保持**戒备**。 |

**同义表达**：*watchful, attentive, prepared, vigilant*

### 2. **spoiled** (形容词)

> **定义**：gone bad
>
> **中文**：变质的；坏掉的

|| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **食物** | *I don’t think I should cook with this chicken, it’s got a bad smell. I think it’s **spoiled**.* | 我不觉得应该用这块鸡肉做饭，它闻起来有异味，我想它**变质了**。 |
| **保存** | *The power cut meant all the food in the fridge **spoiled**.* | 停电导致冰箱里所有食物都**变质了**。 |
| **网络攻击** | *The cyberattack left lots of supermarket food **spoiled**.* | 这次网络攻击导致大量超市食品**变质**。 |

**同义表达**：*rotten, bad, off, decayed*

### 3. **vulnerable** (形容词)

> **定义**：open to being hurt, attacked, or damaged
>
> **中文**：易受伤害的；脆弱的；不安全的

|| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **家居** | *Her house is really close to a river, so it’s **vulnerable** to flooding.* | 她家离河很近，因此**容易**被洪水淹没。 |
| **技术** | *Old software is more **vulnerable** to hacking.* | 旧软件更**易**遭到黑客攻击。 |
| **设备** | *Smart fridges are **vulnerable** if not properly secured.* | 智能冰柜如果未妥善保护就是**脆弱**的。 |

**同义表达**：*defenceless, exposed, unprotected, weak*

**反义词**：*protected, secure, safe, strong*
