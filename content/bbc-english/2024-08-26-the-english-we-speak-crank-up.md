### 中文简介

本期节目介绍短语动词 **"crank up"** 的用法。意思是**"调高/增加/强化"**，指增加某事物的强度、音量、力度或努力程度。Feifei 和 Phil 通过邻居音乐太大声、考试压力、马拉松训练、学波兰语等例子展示用法，并解释语法：宾语可置中或置后。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达含义与词源

> **Feifei**: *"It might actually help if you think about an old machine – a 'crank' is a handle that you turn to make something happen. When we use 'crank up', we mean to increase the intensity of something."*
> 
> **Feifei**: *"想象老机器——'crank' 是**曲柄/摇柄**，转动它让机器运作。'crank up' 就是**增加某事物的强度**——像邻居音箱音量调大。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ **词源**：古老机器的**曲柄**（crank），转动启动/加速
- ✅ **本义**：调高音量、*crank up the volume*
- ✅ **引申义**：增加**任何事物的强度**——压力、努力、训练、学习等
- ✅ **隐喻用法**极常见：*crank up the pressure, crank up training, crank up efforts*

---

### 2. 语法重点：宾语位置灵活

> **Phil**: *"With phrasal verbs, it's always important to know where to put the direct object. With 'crank up' you can either put it in between 'crank' and 'up', or you can put it afterwards."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"短语动词要注意宾语位置。'crank up' 宾语可**置中**也可**置后**。"*

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **crank up + 宾语** | *crank up the volume* | 调高音量 |
| **crank + 宾语 + up** | *crank the heating up* | 调高暖气 |

**规则**：
- ✅ 宾语为**名词短语**时：两种位置均可
- ⚠️ 宾语为**代词**时：必须置中 —— *crank **it** up* (不可 *crank up it*)

### 4. 同义/相关表达对比

| 表达 | 含义 | 语域/细微差别 |
|------|------|--------------|
| **crank up** | 增加强度/音量/努力 | 非正式、形象、口语高频 |
| **turn up** | 调高（音量/温度等） | 较中性，*turn up the volume* |
| **ramp up** | 逐步大幅增加 | 商业/项目管理常用 |
| **step up** | 加强/提升 | 侧重行动升级 |
| **intensify** | 强化/加剧 | 正式、书面 |
| **amp up** | 加大马力/强度 | 口语、非正式 |

## 批判性思考题

1. **机械隐喻的认知考古学**：*crank (曲柄)* 从蒸汽机/早期汽车启动曲柄 → *crank up (启动/加速)* → 抽象强度增加。这种 *"mechanical metaphor"* 如何反映工业时代对 *"effort as energy input"* (努力即能量输入) 的建模？

2. **短语动词的可分性与加工负荷**：*crank up* 属于 **separable phrasal verb**，宾语位置灵活。心理语言学中 *"particle placement"* 如何受 *NP length (名词短语长度)*、*definiteness (指定性)*、*pronoun vs noun* 影响？(*Hawkins's Early Immediate Constituents, Wasow's Performance-Grammar Correspondence*)

3. **强度标量的语用校准**：*crank up the pressure/volume/efforts* 暗示 **scalar implicature (标量蕴含)** —— 存在可调节的强度刻度。如何用英语表达 *"fine-grained control" (精细控制)* vs *"binary on/off"*？(*gradable vs non-gradable adjectives*)

4. **跨域映射的系统性**：*crank up* 可用于音量/压力/训练/学习/暖气/速度/生产/营销/创新。这种 **domain-general intensity verb** 如何与 *domain-specific* 动词共存？(*increase, boost, escalate, magnify, heighten, amplify*)

5. **Crank 的多义网络**：*crank (n. 曲柄/怪人/v. 转动/毒品俚语)*、*crank call (恶作剧电话)*、*crank out (大量生产)*、*crank up (增加强度)*。如何用 *"polysemy network"* 分析 *crank* 族的语义辐射？

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Feifei**: Hello and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain phrases used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them too. I'm Feifei, and I'm here with Phil.

**Phil**: Hello. How are you, Feifei? You look tired.

**Feifei**: Oh, that's a bit rude! Although, it is true – I have a new neighbour. Every time I try to go to sleep, my neighbour **cranks up** the volume of his music. It's driving me crazy.

**Phil**: Oh no! But that does give us a phrasal verb to learn in this programme – '**crank up**'. It can mean 'turn up', but can you tell us a bit more about it?

**Feifei**: Yes. So, it might actually help if you think about an old machine – a **'crank'** is a handle that you turn to make something happen. When we use '**crank up**', we mean to **increase the intensity** of something – like the volume on my neighbour's speakers.

**Phil**: And it's not just volume. It can be the intensity of anything. We often use it **metaphorically**, so you can talk about **cranking up** your efforts to do something. So, if I really want to get fit, I need to **crank up** the level of my workouts.

**Feifei**: Yes, that's right. Here are some examples of people using '**crank up**'.

**Examples**:
> I'm doing a course at the moment, and my teacher has brought forward the date of the exam, and that's really **cranked up** the pressure. I've got so much to study in no time at all.

> I've got a half marathon coming up, so I've really got to **crank up** my training.

> I'm going to Poland. I need to **crank up** my efforts to learn Polish.

**Feifei**: So, '**crank up**' is a phrasal verb. Phil, what else do we need to learn about it?

**Phil**: OK, with phrasal verbs, it's always important to know where to put the direct object. With '**crank up**' you can either put it in between '**crank**' and '**up**', or you can put it afterwards.

**Feifei**: So, when it's cold, you can **crank the heating up**, or you can **crank up the heating**.

**Phil**: Hopefully, this episode will help you **crank up** your vocabulary learning efforts.

**Feifei**: Yes, and we'll be back next time with another useful English phrase.

**Phil**: See you then. Bye!
