## 详细词汇解析

### 1. **turnaround** (名词)

> **定义**：a dramatic change, often successful

> **中文**：扭转局面；急转直下；戏剧性变化

|| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|------|
| **体育** | *After losing their first three games, hiring a new coach led to a **turnaround** in the team's season.* | 连输三场后，聘请新教练带来了球队赛季的**扭转局面**。 |
| **商业** | *The new CEO brought about a **turnaround** in the company's fortunes.* | 新CEO带来了公司命运的**扭转局面**。 |
| **政治** | *Lula's election marks a political **turnaround** for Brazil.* | 卢拉当选标志着巴西政治的一次**扭转局面**。 |

**同义表达**：*comeback, reversal, recovery, swing*

### 2. **progressive** (名词/形容词)

> **定义**：someone who supports liberal, left-wing ideas, seeking to change society

> **中文**：进步派人士；支持自由派左翼思想的人（名词）；进步的；先进的（形容词）

|| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|------|
| **政治观点** | **Progressives** want to see the government introduce more liberal policies. | **进步派人士**希望政府引入更多自由派政策。 |
| **社会改革** | *She is a **progressive** who believes in social change.* | 她是一位相信社会变革的**进步人士**。 |
| **政策** | *The party has adopted more **progressive** tax policies.* | 该党已采取更为**进步**的税收政策。 |

**同义表达**：*liberal, left-wing, reformist, radical*

### 3. **dismayed** (形容词)

> **定义**：feeling sad, very disappointed or worried

> **中文**：沮丧的；失望的；担忧的；惊慌的

|| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|------|
| **公司新闻** | The staff were **dismayed** at the news that the company was to close. | 员工们得知公司即将关闭的消息后感到**沮丧**。 |
| **选举结果** | Investors were **dismayed** by the election outcome. | 投资者对选举结果感到**失望**。 |
| **天气** | We were **dismayed** by the sudden storm. | 突如其来的暴风雨让我们感到**担忧**。 |

**同义表达**：*disappointed, worried, upset, alarmed, distressed*

**反义词**：*pleased, delighted, encouraged, reassured*
