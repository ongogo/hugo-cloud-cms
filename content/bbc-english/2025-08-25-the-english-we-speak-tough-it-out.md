### 中文简介

本期节目介绍表达 **"tough it out"** 的用法。意思是**"咬牙坚持/硬撑过去/忍耐度过"**，指在痛苦或困难情况下**坚持不放弃、不改变计划**。Feifei 和 Phil 通过足球受伤、工作拖欠工资、没吃早餐、没热水、没空调等例子展示用法，并讲解语法：固定搭配 **tough it out**，源自 **phrasal verb tough something out**。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达含义

> **Phil**: *"If you tough it out, then you work through a painful or difficult situation. You stay strong, and don't change your plans."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"如果你 **tough it out**，就是**在痛苦或困难中坚持走出来**。你保持坚强，不改变计划。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ **固定搭配**：**tough it out**（it 固定在中间，不可替换）
- ✅ **核心义**：**忍耐度过**困难/痛苦，**不放弃、不改变计划**
- ✅ **适用范围**：身体疼痛、心理压力、工作困境、生活不便等
- ✅ **语气**：积极、鼓励、坚韧

---

### 2. 语法重点：固定结构与词源

> **Phil**: *"As a phrase, we always put 'it' in the middle, between 'tough' and 'out'. Now, it originally comes from the phrasal verb 'tough something out' – and you can tough something out or tough out something."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"这个短语中，'it' **固定在中间**，在 'tough' 和 'out' 之间。它源自短语动词 'tough something out'——你可以 tough something out，也可以 tough out something。"*

**语法规则**：
| 形式 | 结构 | 例句 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **固定习语** | **tough it out** | *I can tough it out.* | 最常用，it 不可替换 |
| **原始短语动词** | **tough sth out** | *tough the pain out* | 可分离，宾语可置中/置后 |
| **原始短语动词** | **tough out sth** | *tough out the pain* | 宾语置后 |

**关键提示**：
- ⚠️ **tough it out** 中的 **it 固定**，不能说 *tough the situation out*（需用原始短语动词形式）
- ✅ 过去式：**toughed it out**
- ✅ 进行时：**toughing it out**

### 4. 同义/相关表达

| 表达 | 含义 | 语域/细微差别 |
|------|------|--------------|
| **tough it out** | 咬牙坚持、硬撑过去 | 非正式、口语高频、鼓励语气 |

| **grit one's teeth** | 咬牙坚持 | 形象、强调意志力 |
| **hang in there** | 坚持住、别放弃 | 口语鼓励语 |

## 批判性思考题

1. **Tough 的语义演变**：*tough* 原义"坚硬/难切" → "难对付" → "坚强/能吃苦" → 动词用法 *tough it out*。这种 **adjective → verb 零派生** 如何体现英语 *"verbing"* 的生产力？(*conversion, zero derivation*)

2. **It 的固定化机制**：*tough it out* 中 *it* 为何固定化？对比 *stick it out, ride it out, rough it out*。这种 *"dummy it"* 在英语习语中如何形成 *"lexicalized frames"*？(*idiom fixation, syntactic frozenness*)

3. **韧性叙事的文化政治学**：*"tough it out"* 体现 *"individual resilience"* (个人韧性) 叙事，可能掩盖 *"structural inequality"* (结构性不平等)——如职场剥削、住房恶劣。如何用英语批判 *"resilience rhetoric"* 与 *"systemic change"* 的张力？(*neoliberal subjectivity, responsibilization*)

4. **身心统一的隐喻**：*tough it out* 既用于身体疼痛（足球受伤）也用于心理/情境困境（工作、饥饿、酷热）。这种 *"embodied metaphor"* 如何体现 *"pain"* 的广义化？(*conceptual metaphor theory, PAIN IS A STRUGGLE*)

5. **学习策略的元隐喻**：Feifei 结尾 *"It can be hard to learn phrasal verbs, but if you tough it out, you'll get there."* 将语言学习框架为 *"endurance trial"*。这种 *"learning as suffering"* 隐喻利弊如何？*growth mindset vs deficit framing*

**Feifei**: Hello and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain phrases used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them too. I'm Feifei.

**Phil**: And I'm Phil. Ow!

**Feifei**: You look like you're in pain, Phil. Are you OK?

**Phil**: I'll be OK. I hurt myself playing football, but I'm sure I can **tough it out**.

**Feifei**: **Tough it out**? Are you sure you can, Phil? Anyway, that's a great phrase to learn. What does '**tough it out**' mean?

**Phil**: If you **tough it out**, then you work through a painful or difficult situation. You stay strong, and don't change your plans.

**Feifei**: And it's not just for physical pains like yours. We can use it for difficult situations. For example, you might be working for a company that can't afford to employ enough people and you just have to **tough it out** until they've got some more money.

**Phil**: Let's hear some other people using this phrase.

**Examples**:
> I am so hungry. I didn't have any time to eat breakfast but I'm going to have to **tough it out** till my lunch break.

> Oh no! There's no hot water and I really need a shower. I'm going to have to **tough it out**.

> Oh, there's no air conditioning in this building. I'm absolutely boiling! I guess I'll just have to **tough it out**.

**Feifei**: We've been learning the phrase '**tough it out**' to mean work through a difficult situation and not let it stop you.

**Phil**: As a phrase, we always put 'it' in the middle, between 'tough' and 'out'. Now, it originally comes from the phrasal verb 'tough something out' – and you can tough something out or tough out something.

**Feifei**: Yes. It can be hard to learn phrasal verbs, but if you **tough it out**, you'll get there.

**Phil**: And make sure you come back for the next programme. We'll see you then!
