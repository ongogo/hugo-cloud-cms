### The Story

A nursery in Chester, northwest England, has opened inside a retirement village. The nursery brings together the oldest people — those who are retired — and the youngest — children who attend nursery — sharing a library, cafeteria and garden. Older residents, called 'grand-friends' by the children, lead activities like dance and crafts. The charity Ready Generations runs the nursery and works with universities to study how mixing generations affects health and wellbeing. Studies show these projects can reduce loneliness, improve health and wellbeing, and boost learning and creativity.

### 3. **such and such** (短语)

> **定义**：a way to talk about something without giving a specific example
> **中文**：某某；诸如此类；诸如此类事物

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **日常活动** | *Staff phoned to tell him about the daily activities, which he refers to as **such and such**.* | 工作人员打电话告诉他日常活动，他称之为**诸如此类**的事情。 |
| **不具体** | *We'll do **such and such** at the meeting.* | 我们会在会上做**某某事情**。 |
| **举例替代** | *He talked about **such and such** without naming anyone.* | 他谈了**某某事**，但没点名。 |

**同义表达**：*so-and-so, whatever, things like that*

## 批判性思考题

1. **代际交往的好处**：节目提到跨代交往能减少孤独感、改善健康并提升创造力。你觉得不同年龄的人在一起活动，最大的好处是什么？*generation, loneliness, wellbeing, creativity*
2. **打破刻板印象**：大家常觉得年轻人爱闹、老人安静，但托儿所里情况往往相反。你生活中遇到过哪些被打破的年龄刻板印象？*break down stereotypes, lively, generation*
3. **填补空白**：Ian 在妻子去世后通过参与托儿所活动**fill a void**。当你经历失去或孤独时，什么事情能帮助你感觉好一些？*fill a void, loss, feel better*
4. **非正式消息**：Myra 是**hear on the grapevine**得知 Ian 教孩子做纸飞机的。非正式渠道传播的消息可靠吗？你信不过这种“传闻”吗？*hear on the grapevine, rumor, informal*
5. **测验反思**：1982 年出生的人属于千禧一代（Millennial）而非 X 一代。你觉得“代际划分”是否准确？不同代际的人真的差异很大吗？*generation, Millennial, Generation X, Gen Z*
