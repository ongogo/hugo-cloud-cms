## 本周问题

> **By how much has Earth's temperature increased since 1970?**
> 
> 自 1970 年以来，地球温度上升了多少？
> 
> a) Around 0.5°C
> 
> b) **Around 1°C** ✅
> 
> c) Around 2°C

**答案**：**Around 1°C**（约 1 摄氏度）。这是全球陆地和海洋表面的平均值。北极升温速度是平均值的 3 倍。仅 1°C 的平均升温就能导致热浪、山火、洪水等极端事件大幅增加。

## 重点词汇表

| 单词/短语 | 英文释义 | 中文释义 | 来源语境 |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| **likelihood** | how probable something is to happen | 可能性；概率 | Darren Bett 讨论洪水风险 |
| **landslide** | where earth, rocks, etc move quickly downhill | 山体滑坡；滑坡 | 暴雨后地质灾害 |
| **mudslide** | a type of landslide where liquid mud moves quickly downhill | 泥石流；泥浆流 | 比 landslide 更快、更液态 |
| **storm surge** | a sudden rise in sea level that moves inland | 风暴潮；风暴增水 | 风暴推动海水内涌 |
| **sweep in** | move in quickly and powerfully | 横扫而入；猛烈袭来 | 风暴潮/风暴内陆推进 |
| **casualty** | someone killed or injured in an accident, attack or incident | 伤亡人员；死伤者 | 极端天气造成的人员伤亡 |

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心区别：Weather vs Climate

> **Sarah Keith-Lucas**: *"The weather is what you see and feel every day. Climate is more long-term."*
> 
> **莎拉·基思-卢卡斯**: *"天气是你每天看到和感受到的。气候是更长期的。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ **Weather** = 短期、日常体验 (today's rain, tomorrow's sun)
- ✅ **Climate** = 长期统计模式 (30 年平均)
- ✅ 气候变化通过**改变长期统计**表现为**更频繁/更强烈的天气**

---

### 2. 热带风暴三重奏：同一现象，不同名称

> **Sarah**: *"Hurricanes are found in the Atlantic, Cyclones in the Indian Ocean and Typhoons in the West Pacific."*
> 
> **莎拉**: *"飓风见于大西洋，气旋见于印度洋，台风见于西太平洋。"*

| 名称 | 区域 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| **Hurricane** (飓风) | 大西洋、东北太平洋 | Saffir-Simpson 分级 |
| **Cyclone** (气旋) | 印度洋、南太平洋 | 澳洲/印度分级制 |
| **Typhoon** (台风) | 西北太平洋 | 中国/日本/菲律宾常用 |

**气候变化影响**：全球变暖 → 海水升温 → **风暴更具破坏性**、**移动更慢** → 雨量更大、持续更久

---

### 3. 风暴减速的连锁效应

> **Darren Bett**: *"Scientists also think storms are slowing down... more likelihood of flooding and landslides and also mudslides."*
> 
> **达伦·贝特**: *"科学家认为风暴在减速……洪水、山体滑坡、泥石流的可能性更大。"*

**机制链**：
```
全球变暖 → 海洋升温 → 风暴移动减速
    ↓
同一区域停留时间更长
    ↓
风力持续更久 + 降雨持续更久
    ↓
洪水风险 ↑ + 山体滑坡/泥石流风险 ↑
```

---

### 4. 致命要素：风暴潮

> **Darren Bett**: *"Storm surge... produces more casualties than any other [element of a storm]."*
> 
> **达伦·贝特**: *"风暴潮……产生的伤亡比风暴任何其他要素都多。"*

> **Sarah Keith-Lucas**: *"A storm surge is where the sea level rises quickly pushed by the strong winds caused by a storm. This water comes onto land with an enormous amount of force, and storm surges can be up to 10 metres high."*
> 
> **莎拉·基思-卢卡斯**: *"风暴潮是强风推动海平面急剧上升。海水以巨大力量涌向陆地，高度可达 10 米。"*

**加剧因素**：
- ✅ 海平面上升 → 基准线更高 → 风暴潮更深、更远
- ✅ 海水升温 → 风暴更强 → 推水能力更强

---

### 5. 地质次生灾害对比

| 现象 | 定义 | 关键特征 |
|------|------|----------|
| **Landslide** (山体滑坡) | 岩石/泥土快速下滑 | 固体为主，速度快 |
| **Mudslide** (泥石流) | 液态泥浆快速下滑 | 液体为主，**更快**、冲击力更强 |
| **触发条件** | 暴雨、地震、人为开挖 | 饱和土壤失稳 |

## 批判性思考题

1. **1°C 的非线性放大**：全球平均升温 1°C，为何极端事件频率/强度远超线性增长？(*Clausius-Clapeyron relation, moisture holding capacity, energy budget*)

2. **风暴命名的地缘政治**：*Hurricane/Cyclone/Typhoon* 同一物理现象，不同名称反映了何种气象史/殖民史？统一术语 *tropical cyclone* 推广面临何种阻力？

3. **风暴减速机制争议**：*storm stalling* 归因于 *weakened steering currents / Arctic amplification / expanded subtropical highs*。当前气候模型对这一机制的共识度如何？

4. **风暴潮与海平面上升的叠加风险**：*10m storm surge + 0.3m SLR (since 1993) + high tide + wave setup*。沿海城市防灾标准如何动态调整？(*design flood elevation, freeboard, adaptation pathways*)

5. **Casualty 统计的伦理维度**：*casualty* 合并死亡/受伤，掩盖了脆弱群体(老人/儿童/贫困/残障)的不成比例风险。灾害社会学关键词汇： *vulnerability, exposure, coping capacity, differential impact*。

**Phil**: So, we had the word **casualty**, and this is used to talk about people killed or injured by an accident, a military attack, or in this case an extreme weather event.

**Sarah Keith-Lucas**: Darren talked about **storm surges**. A **storm surge** is where the sea level rises quickly pushed by the strong winds caused by a storm. **Phil**: We heard about the storm **sweeping inland**. Something **sweeping in** is moving quickly and powerfully. And you can hear more from Darren in the videos on our website. Sarah, how about the answer to the quiz question you asked earlier?

**Sarah Keith-Lucas**: OK, Phil. So, I asked by how much has the world's average temperature increased since 1970.

**Phil**: And I thought that it was around two celsius.

**Sarah Keith-Lucas**: It's actually around one degree celsius. 1C is just an average across all of earth's land and ocean surface. Some places are warming much quicker than this such as the Arctic that's warming 3 times faster than the average rate. Just a small increase in average temperature can lead to a much bigger increase in extreme events such as heatwaves, wildfires and floods.

**Phil**: Oh wow, so that is an important difference. OK, let's recap the vocabulary we've learned, starting with **likelihood**, which is noun that we use to talk how probable or how likely something is to happen.

**Phil**: A **storm surge** is a sudden rise in the sea level, that can move inland causing destruction.

**Sarah Keith-Lucas**: If something **sweeps in**, it moves in very fast and very powerfully.

**Phil**: And finally, a **casualty** is someone who is injured or killed by an accident, attack, or something like an extreme weather event. Once again, our six minutes are up.
