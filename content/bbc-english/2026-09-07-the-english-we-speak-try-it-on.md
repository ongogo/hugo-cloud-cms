### 中文简介

本期节目介绍表达 **"try it on"** 的两个含义（除常见的"试穿衣服"外）：
1. **试图欺骗** — 编造谎言以逃避麻烦/责任
2. **试探底线** — 故意行为不当，测试对方能容忍到什么程度

Feifei 和 Phil 通过请假看球赛、借钱实为买包裹、餐厅不找零、房东涨租、青少年玩手机等生动例子展示如何使用，并指出该表达常用于否定句式挑战对方 (*Don't try it on!*)。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达两大含义

> **Phil**: *"One meaning of 'try it on' is to try to deceive someone so you don't get in trouble."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"'Try it on' 的一个含义是**试图欺骗某人**，以免陷入麻烦。"*

> **Phil**: *"Another meaning of 'try it on' is to behave badly to test someone's limits."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"'Try it on' 的另一个含义是**故意行为不当，以试探某人的底线**。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ 含义 1：**欺骗/找借口** — *She's just trying it on* (她在编造谎言)
- ✅ 含义 2：**试探底线** — *teenagers trying it on* (青少年试探父母底线)
- ✅ **常用否定句挑战** — *Don't try it on!* / *I know you're not..., don't try it on.*
- ✅ 区别于字面义 *"try on clothes"* (试穿衣服)

---

### 2. 语法重点：常用否定句式

| 形式 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **Don't try it on!** | 直接命令制止 | *Don't try it on! I've told you to put your phone away.* |
| **I know..., don't try it on.** | 表明看穿谎言 | *I know you're not going to the airport, don't try it on.* |
| **She's/They're just trying it on.** | 陈述判断 | *She's just trying it on.* / *They're definitely trying it on.* |

---

### 3. 课文实例解析

#### Example 1: 谎称没钱借卡实为买包裹
> *"My sister said she's got no money and asked to borrow my credit card to buy some food from the shops but I saw a massive parcel with her name on it. She's just **trying it on**."*
> 
> 妹妹说没钱借信用卡买吃的，但我看到巨大包裹写着她名字。她纯属**编造借口/欺骗**。

#### Example 2: 餐厅不找零
> *"The waiter... didn't bring me back any change after I paid. They said that they'd just forgotten, but I think they were **trying it on**."*
> 
> 服务员付款后不找零，说忘了，我觉得他们在**故意占便宜/试探能不能赖账**。

#### Example 3: 房东涨租
> *"My landlord raised my rent by £100 without telling me. They're definitely **trying it on**."*
> 
> 房东没通知就涨租 100 镑，绝对是在**试探底线/耍赖**。

#### Example 4: 青少年玩手机
> *"Teenagers are notorious for sometimes **trying it on** to see what their parents will let them do, like using their phones after we've told them to stop."*
> 
> 青少年**以此出名**，他们**故意违规玩手机**，看父母到底能容忍到什么程度。

## 批判性思考题

1. **欺骗 vs 试探的界限**：含义 1(*deceive*) 与含义 2(*test limits*) 在实际语境中常重叠。如何用英语区分 *"He's lying"* vs *"He's testing boundaries"*？(*white lie vs boundary pushing*)

2. **否定句式的语用力量**：*Don't try it on!* 比 *Stop lying!* 或 *Stop misbehaving!* 语气如何不同？它包含了"我看穿你了"的元认知层面。

3. **权力不对称场景**：房东-租客、服务员-顾客、父母-子女、雇主-雇员。在这些不对称关系中，*try it on* 通常由谁对谁使用？反映了什么社会动态？

4. **跨文化对比**：中文"找借口""撒谎""耍赖""试探底线""蹬鼻子上脸""得寸进尺"对应 *try it on* 的不同侧面。如何根据语境选择最贴切的中文对应词？

5. **幽默化解冲突**：Feifei 结尾 *"We're not just trying it on!"* 用自指幽默化解教学严肃感。教学/沟通中如何用自嘲建立亲和力？

**Feifei**: Hello and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain phrases and expressions used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them too. I'm Feifei.

**Phil**: And I'm Phil.

**Phil**: No, I'm leaving early. I'm…. erm…. ah…. going to the airport. I'm going on holiday and I need get there early.

**Feifei**: Wait a minute, you're not going on holiday. I don't believe you. You're just **trying it on**. That important football match is on this afternoon. You just want to go and watch that.

**Phil**: Maybe…. Ok, if you promise not to tell anyone else, I'll explain what '**try it on**' means here.

**Feifei**: OK, deal. Everyone knows '**try it on**' can be used to talk about clothes. But this is different, isn't it?

**Phil**: Yes, this is about lying. One meaning of '**try it on**' is to try to deceive someone so you don't get in trouble. If someone lies to get out of work early – then they are '**trying it on**'.

**Feifei**: Yes, it's an expression that we often use to challenge someone. This means we often use it in the negative. I know you're not going to the airport, **don't try it on**.

**Phil**: Let's hear some more people using '**try it on**' in this way.

**Examples**:
> My sister said she's got no money and asked to borrow my credit card to buy some food from the shops but I saw a massive parcel with her name on it. She's just **trying it on**.

> So, the waiter in the restaurant I went to last night didn't bring me back any change after I paid. They said that they'd just forgotten, but I think they were **trying it on**.

> My landlord raised my rent by £100 without telling me. They're definitely **trying it on**.

**Feifei**: We've been learning the expression '**try it on**' to talk about trying to deceive someone.

**Phil**: Yes. Another meaning of '**try it on**' is to behave badly to test someone's limits.

**Feifei**: So, Phil. You've got teenage children, haven't you?

**Phil**: Yes, teenagers are notorious for sometimes **trying it on** to see what their parents will let them do, like using their phones after we've told them to stop. It means I need to say "**Don't try it on!** I've told you to put your phone away".

**Feifei**: OK. That's it for this programme but join us again next time. We really can help you improve your English. We're not just **trying it on**!
