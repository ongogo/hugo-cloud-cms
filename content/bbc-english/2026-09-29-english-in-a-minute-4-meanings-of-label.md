### 中文简介

本期节目讲解单词 **'label'** 的 **4 个含义**：
1. **标签/说明** — 食品包装上的成分表、分类标识
2. **给人贴标签/定型判断** — 基于单一特征对人进行刻板印象描述
3. **唱片公司** — 音乐产业中的 record label
4. **坐标轴标签** — 图表中解释变量含义的 axis label

---

## 4 个含义详解

### 1. **information about something** (标签/说明/标识)

> **Becca**: *"A label tells you information, such as the name, description or category of something."*
> 
> **Becca**: *"标签告诉你信息，如名称、描述或类别。"*

**例句**：
- *I always check the **labels** of food in the supermarket.* (我总在超市查看食品**标签**。)
- *Can you check if that has a gluten-free **label** on it? I'm allergic!* (能查一下那上面有没有**无麸质标识**？我过敏！)

**常见搭配**：
- *food label* (食品标签)
- *warning label* (警告标签)
- *price label* (价签)
- *nutrition label* (营养成分表)

---

### 2. **judgement about someone** (给人贴标签/定型判断)

> **Becca**: *"If you label someone, you give them a general, stereotypical description, usually based on only one part of their character."*
> 
> **Becca**: *"给某人**贴标签**，就是基于其性格的某一点，给出一个笼统、刻板的描述。"*

**例句**：
- *You shouldn't **label** people just because of their style.* (别因穿衣风格就**给人贴标签**。)
- *I've been **labelled** as lazy just because I don't enjoy football...* (我被**贴上懒惰标签**只因不爱踢球……)
- *It's not a fair **label**.* (这**标签**不公平。)

**语法注意**：
- 动词：*label someone (as) + 形容词/名词*
- 被动常用：*be labelled as...*
- 名词用法：*a fair/unfair label*

**同义表达**：
- *pigeonhole someone* (把人归类/刻板印象化)
- *stereotype* (刻板印象)
- *categorise* (归类)

---

### 3. **music company** (唱片公司)

> **Becca**: *"A **record label** is a company that produces and distributes music."*
> 
> **Becca**: *"**唱片公司**是制作和发行音乐的公司。"*

**例句**：
- *My favourite band has just signed a contract with a famous **record label**.* (我最爱的乐队刚和一家著名**唱片公司**签约。)
- *That singer has just signed with a major **label** — she's going to be famous soon!* (那歌手刚签了**大厂**，马上要红了！)

**行业术语**：
- *major label* (大唱片公司：Universal, Sony, Warner)
- *independent label / indie label* (独立唱片公司)
- *sign to a label* (签约唱片公司)
- *label mate* (同门艺人)

---

### 4. **(axis) label** (坐标轴标签)

> **Becca**: *"An **axis label** lets you know what variables graph is measuring, which can help to show a trend in data."*
> 
> **Becca**: *"**坐标轴标签**让你知道图表在测量什么变量，有助于显示数据趋势。"*

**例句**：
- *The **axis labels** tell us that this graph shows the number of people shopping with us in each month of this year.* (坐标轴标签告诉我们这图表显示今年每月购物人数。)
- *Don't forget to **label** your axes in the question about graph, otherwise you will lose a mark!* (别忘给坐标轴**加标签**，否则扣分！)

**学术/数据可视化语境**：
- *x-axis label / y-axis label* (x/y 轴标签)
- *label the axes* (标注坐标轴)
- *data label* (数据标签)

## 批判性思考题

1. **多义词的认知加工**：*label* 从物理标签 → 抽象分类 → 社会评判 → 机构名称 → 数据标注。这种 *"semantic extension"* (语义延伸) 路径如何体现 *"metaphor"* (隐喻) 与 *"metonymy"* (转喻) 的交织？*category mistake* (范畴错误) 何时发生？

2. **"贴标签"的社会权力学**：*label someone* 隐含 *"labeller" (贴标签者)* 拥有 *epistemic authority* (认识论权威)。*self-labeling* (自我标签) vs *external labelling* (他者标签) 在身份建构中如何博弈？*identity politics*

3. **音乐产业的 Label 迁移**：*record label* 本指唱片中心标签 → *metonymy* 转指公司。数字化时代 *label* 功能如何重构？*distribution, marketing, A&R, rights management* 何者仍属 label 核心？

4. **数据可视化的标签伦理**：*axis label* 缺失或误导 → *chart junk* / *misleading visualization*。如何用英语表达 *"axis manipulation, truncated axis, dual-axis deception"*？*data literacy*

5. **跨语言 "label" 对应词**：中文"标签/贴标签/唱片公司/坐标轴标题" 四义分离。日文 *"レーベル" (label/唱片公司)*、韩文 *"레이블"* 如何对应？*loanword polysemy transfer*

## 完整讲稿记录

### Transcript

**Becca**: This is a **label**. Do you know these other meanings of 'label'? A **label** can give information like a name, description or category.

**Example**: I always check the **labels** of food in the supermarket.

**Becca**: We can **label** people based on a judgement we have about them.

**Example**: You shouldn't **label** people just because of their style. They could wear dark clothes and have a bright personality.

**Becca**: In the music industry, a **label** is a company that produces and distributes music.

**Example**: My favourite band has just signed a contract with a famous **record label**. I'm so proud of them!

**Becca**: And finally, with data, an **axis label** gives us context for the trends on a graph.

**Example**: The x-axis shows the number of people shopping with us, and the y-axis shows the months of this year.

**Becca**: Which meanings of 'label' are new for you?
