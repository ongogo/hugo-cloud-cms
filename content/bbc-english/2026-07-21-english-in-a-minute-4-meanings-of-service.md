### 中文简介

本期节目讲解高频词 **'service'** 的 **4 个不同含义**（除常见的"服务/服务业"外）：
1. **固定班次的火车/公交车** — *the 9.15 service*
2. **机械维修保养** — *car service, annual service*
3. **网球等球拍运动的发球** — *service / serve*
4. **高速公路服务区** — *services (复数)*

Phil 通过生动例句快速演示每个用法，适合碎片化学习。

---

## 4 个含义详解

### 1. **a specific train or bus** — 固定班次的火车/公交车

> **用法**：*the [时间] service* (名词)
> 
> **含义**：固定时间发出的特定班次列车或公交车

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Sorry, can you tell me which platform I need for the **9.15 service**?* | 对不起，能告诉我 9:15 **这班车** 在哪个站台吗？ |
| *We're afraid that today's **10.15 service** to Manchester has been cancelled.* | 恐怕今天 10:15 **开往曼彻斯特的班次** 已取消。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 交通运输专用语
- ✅ 强调"按时刻表运行的固定班次"
- ✅ 常用结构：*the [time] service to [destination]*

---

### 2. **maintaining something mechanical** — 机械维修保养

> **用法**：*a service / for a service / book a service* (名词)
> 
> **含义**：对机械设备（汽车、家电、供暖系统等）进行的定期检查维护

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *OK, so you're bringing your **car in for its annual service**? Great, I'll look at it now.* | 好的，你把车送来做**年度保养**？好的，我马上检查。 |
| *Can I **book a service** for my washing machine?* | 我能预约一下洗衣机的**维修/保养**吗？ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 常用搭配：*car service, annual service, regular service, book a service*
- ✅ 相关动词：*service (vt.)* — *The engineer serviced the boiler.*
- ✅ 同义：*maintenance, check-up, tune-up (美式)*

---

### 3. **A shot in tennis** — 网球/球拍运动的发球

> **用法**：*a service / serve* (名词)
> 
> **含义**：网球、羽毛球、乒乓球等球拍运动中开始一分的发球动作

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *She's been practising her **service** and now she's one of the best.* | 她一直在练习**发球**，现在发球技术数一数二。 |
| *A good **service** is one of the most important things to learn in badminton.* | 好的**发球**是学羽毛球最重要的基本功之一。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 可与 *serve* 互换使用
- ✅ 网球术语：*first service, second service, service game, break of service*
- ✅ 动词用法：*to serve*

---

### 4. **A place to buy fuel or food** — 高速公路服务区

> **用法**：*services* (复数名词，英式英语)
> 
> **含义**：高速公路上供加油、用餐、休息的综合服务设施（美式常说 *rest area, service plaza*）

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *I'm starving! How long until we get to the **services**?* | 我饿死了！多久能到**服务区**？ |
| *Make sure you have enough petrol to get to the next **services**.* | 确保油够开到下一个**服务区**。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ **通常用复数** *services* (即使指单个地点)
- ✅ **英式英语**常用，美式对应 *rest area, service plaza, travel center*
- ✅ 设施通常包括：加油站、餐厅、厕所、便利店

## 批判性思考题

1. **核心语义网络**：*service* 原义"服务/服侍"，如何辐射出"定期班次(为乘客服务)"、"维修保养(为设备服务)"、"发球(开启比赛服务)"、"服务区(为司机服务)"四大义项？画出语义辐射图。

2. **单复数语义差异**：含义 4 *services* 强制复数，含义 1-3 用单数 *a service*。英语中还有哪些"复数形式表单一实体/地点"的词？(*premises, headquarters, outskirts, surroundings*)

3. **英美用法差异**：含义 4 *services* (英式) vs *rest area / service plaza* (美式)。交通设施词汇还有哪些英美差异？(*motorway/freeway, petrol/gas, car park/parking lot, roundabout/traffic circle*)

4. **体育术语跨域迁移**：*service* 从网球 → 羽毛球/乒乓球/壁球/匹克尔球。体育术语跨项目复用的其他例子？(*serve, volley, smash, ace, deuce, love, set, match*)

5. **服务业词汇簇**：*service, serve, server, servicing, serviceable, self-service, full-service, after-sales service, customer service*。梳理 *service* 词族的词法生产力。

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Phil**: How well do you really know the word 'service'? You probably know this meaning, but what about these? A service can be a train or a bus – it's the one that comes at the same time every day.

**Example**: *Sorry, can you tell me which platform I need for the 9.15 service?*

**Phil**: A service can be when someone maintains something mechanical for you. Heating systems and cars often need regular services.

**Example**: *OK. So, you're bringing your car in for its annual service? Great. I'll look at it now.*

**Phil**: We also call it a serve, but a service can be what you do in tennis or any other racquet sport to start or restart the game.

**Example**: *She's been practising her service and now she's one of the best.*

**Phil**: Services are also where you stop to get food or petrol on a long drive.

**Example**: *I'm starving! How long until we get to the services?*

**Phil**: Try to remember these four ways to use the word 'service'.
