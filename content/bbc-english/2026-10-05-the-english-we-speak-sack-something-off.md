### 中文简介

本期节目介绍短语动词 **"sack something off"** 的用法。意思是**"取消/放弃/不做了"**，指**因懒惰或宁愿做别事而取消计划、停止做某事**。Feifei 和 Georgie 通过健身、培训、巴黎徒步团、买菜做饭等例子展示用法，并讲解语法：可分离短语动词，宾语可置中或置后。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达含义

> **Georgie**: *"Well, in this case, sack off plans means cancel plans. More broadly, it can mean to stop or avoid doing something because you feel too lazy or you'd rather be doing something else."*
> 
> **Georgie**: *"この場合，sack off plans 就是取消计划。更广泛地说，指因**太懒**或**宁愿做别事**而**停止或避免做某事**。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ **非正式、口语**、英式英语常用
- ✅ 核心义：**取消/放弃**，带有 *"can't be bothered"* (懒得/不想麻烦) 色彩
- ✅ 两大使用场景：
  1. **取消社交安排** (*sack off plans*)
  2. **逃避任务/差事** (*sack off training, sack off cooking*)

---

### 2. 语法重点：可分离短语动词

> **Georgie**: *"That's right, and it's a **separable phrasal verb**, which means you put an object at the end of the verb or in the middle. So, you can say 'sack off a plan' or 'sack a plan off'."*
> 
> **Georgie**: *"这是**可分离短语动词**，宾语可放在动词后或中间。*sack off a plan* 或 *sack a plan off* 都行。"*

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **sack off + 宾语** | *sack off the gym* | 取消去健身房 |
| **sack + 宾语 + off** | *sack the gym off* | 取消去健身房 |

**规则**：
- ✅ 宾语为**名词短语**时：两种位置均可
- ⚠️ 宾语为**代词**时：必须置中 —— *sack **it** off* (不可 *sack off it*)

### 4. 同义/相关表达

## 批判性思考题

1. **Sack 的语义演变**：*sack (n. 袋子/解雇/v. 解雇/掠夺)* → *sack off (取消)*。这种 *"semantic bleaching" (语义消色)* 如何从 *"dismissal from employment" (解雇)* → *"self-dismissal from obligation" (自我免除义务)*？*grammaticalization path*

2. **懒惰的合理化修辞**：*sack off* 将 *"procrastination/avoidance" (拖延/回避)* 包装为 *"self-care/prioritization" (自我关怀/优先级管理)*。话语标记 *"I'd rather..."* 如何将 *"laziness" (懒惰)* 重构为 *"preference" (偏好)*？*face-saving strategy*

3. **可分离短语动词的处理成本**：*sack off the plan* vs *sack the plan off*。*particle placement* 受 *NP length, definiteness, pronoun vs noun, information structure* 影响。英式 vs 美式 *blow off* 语法行为异同？

4. **跨文化"取消计划"礼貌策略**：英式 *sack off* 带幽默自嘲；中文"鸽了/放鸽子/爽约/鸽掉" 带负面社交评价；日文 *"ドタキャン" (dotakyan/最后一刻取消)* 如何对应？*cultural scripts of flaking*

5. **数字时代的 Sack Off 成本降低**：*WhatsApp/Line/微信群* 一条消息即可 *sack off* 约会。*low-cost cancellation* 如何改变 *"commitment calibration" (承诺校准)* 与 *"social reliability" (社交可靠度)*？*ghosting, soft ghosting, benching*

### Transcript

**Feifei**: Hello and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain expressions used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them, too! I'm Feifei, and I'm joined by Georgie. How are you?

**Georgie**: Feifei I am exhausted, and sadly I've got plans every night this week. Big error!

**Feifei**: Oh no! What are you going to do?

**Georgie**: Well, I think I'm going to **sack off** some of the bigger plans and just hope that my friends don't mind too much.

**Feifei**: That sounds like a sensible idea. You need to rest! Now, that's a good phrasal verb to learn – **sack something off**. What does it mean?

**Georgie**: Well, in this case, **sack off** plans means cancel plans. More broadly, it can mean to stop or avoid doing something because you feel too lazy or you'd rather be doing something else.

**Feifei**: Right. I'm supposed to be going to the gym later but I'd rather **sack that off** and watch TV.

**Georgie**: Great example! Let's hear more from our BBC Learning English colleagues.

**Examples**:
> The work training is optional and it's so boring, so I decided to **sack it off** and get some coffee instead.

> We booked a walking tour in Paris, but we were so tired after our flight that we decided to **sack it off**.

> I needed to get some ingredients in for dinner, but I was kind of feeling lazy, so I decided to **sack it off** and get a takeaway instead.

**Feifei**: Now, '**sack something off**' is a phrasal verb, isn't it?

**Georgie**: That's right, and it's a **separable phrasal verb**, which means you put an object at the end of the verb or in the middle. So, you can say '**sack off a plan**' or '**sack a plan off**'. Shall we **sack off** the rest of this programme?!

**Feifei**: No, Georgie! We just have to do a recap and then we're done. Don't be lazy!

**Georgie**: You're right, you're right. OK. We learnt '**sack something off**' which means to cancel something, avoid or stop doing something. See you again next time!

**Feifei**: Thanks for joining us. Bye bye!

**Georgie**: Bye.
