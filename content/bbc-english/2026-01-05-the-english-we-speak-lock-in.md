### 中文简介

本期节目介绍表达 **"lock in"** 的用法。意思是**全神贯注、专心致志、排除干扰专注于某事**。Feifei 和 Beth 通过备考、运动比赛、跑步目标、驾照考试、工作赶进度、孩子复习等例子展示如何使用。这是一个非正式表达，常用于朋友家人间，较少用于职场正式场合。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达含义

> **Beth**: *"'Lock in' means focus really hard on something, commit to the tasks and avoid distractions."*
> 
> **Beth**: *'"Lock in" 意思是**全神贯注于某事**，致力于任务并避免干扰。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ **动词短语**：*lock in* / *lock in on sth*
- ✅ **含义**：排除干扰、全力以赴、进入"心流"状态
- ✅ **非正式表达**：常用于朋友、家人间，职场正式场合较少用
- ✅ **常见场景**：备考、运动备战、工作冲刺、目标达成

---

### 2. 两种语法形式

| 形式 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **lock in** (不及物) | 进入专注状态 | *I need to lock in and get this done.* |
| **lock in on + 目标** | 专注于特定目标 | *I need to lock in on my fitness goals.* |
| **be locked in** (形容词) | 处于高度专注状态 | *She's completely locked in right now.* |

---

### 3. 使用场景分类

| 场景 | 例句 | 语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **学术备考** | *I really had to **lock in** last night.* | Beth 西班牙语考试前复习 |
| **运动比赛** | *Players might **lock in** before they play a big match.* | 赛前进入状态 |
| **长期目标** | *I need to **lock in on** my fitness goals.* | Feifei 半程马拉松训练 |
| **技能练习** | *I'm going to have to just **lock in** and do loads of practice.* | 反复失败驾照考试后 |
| **工作冲刺** | *This afternoon I really just need to **lock in** and get some stuff done.* | 白天摸鱼下午赶进度 |
| **青少年复习** | *They're **locking in** for their exams.* | 孩子们晚间备考 |

---

### 4. 语域提示

> **Beth**: *"'Lock in' is informal. You're more likely to use it with friends and family than in a professional setting."*
> 
> **Beth**: *'"Lock in" 是非正式表达。你更可能在朋友家人间使用，而不是职场正式场合。"*

**替代表达（正式场合）**：
- *focus intently on*
- *concentrate fully on*
- *dedicate oneself to*
- *apply oneself to*

## 批判性思考题

1. **Lock in vs Flow state**：*lock in* 强调主动意志力排除干扰，*flow state* 强调沉浸体验的自发性。两者在心理学机制上有何异同？如何用英语区分 *"I locked in"* vs *"I entered a flow state"*？

2. **数字时代的注意力经济**：*lock in* 暗示关闭手机/社交媒体。现代人如何用英语表达"数字排毒/深度工作/番茄工作法/单任务处理"？(*digital detox, deep work, pomodoro technique, single-tasking*)

3. **体育心理学术语**：*lock in, in the zone, game face, mental toughness, clutch performance*。运动员赛前常规中 *lock in* 处于什么位置？如何翻译"赛前锁定状态"？

4. **目标设定框架**：*lock in on goals* 与 SMART 目标、OKR、习惯叠加 如何结合？设计一个 *Lock-in Protocol* 英语清单。

5. **跨文化对比**：中文"锁死""上头""入迷""钻牛角尖""废寝忘食""聚精会神""屏息凝神"对应 *lock in* 的不同侧面。何时用正面/负面词汇？

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Feifei**: Hello and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain phrases used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them too. I'm Feifei, and I'm here with Beth. Hi Beth.

**Beth**: Hi Feifei. I am exhausted. I've been studying for hours before my Spanish exam. I really had to **lock in** last night.

**Feifei**: **Lock in**? You weren't locked in a room, were you?

**Beth**: No! **'Lock in'** means focus really hard on something, commit to the tasks and avoid distractions. I turned off my phone and studied as much as I could.

**Feifei**: I see. So you worked seriously and with concentration. **'Lock in'** is all about intense focus, determination and boosting productivity.

**Beth**: Exactly. All my focus was on my revision – no distractions. You might also hear **'lock in'** about sports. Players might **lock in** before they play a big match.

**Feifei**: Yes. Also, we can **lock in on** our goals. For example, I'm preparing to run a half marathon in three months, so I need to **lock in on** my fitness goals.

**Beth**: Wow. Good luck.

**Feifei**: Yes. Thank you. Let's listen to more examples of **'lock in'**.

**Examples**:
> I really need to stop failing my driving test. I'm going to have to just **lock in** and do loads of practice.

> Right, guys. I've done no work all day, so I've had a coffee, and this afternoon I really just need to **lock in** and get some stuff done.

> I never see my kids in the evening anymore. They're **locking in** for their exams.

**Beth**: **'Lock in'** is informal. You're more likely to use it with friends and family than in a professional setting.

**Feifei**: We can also describe someone as being **'locked in'**, meaning they're doing something with extreme focus.

**Beth**: Well, I was definitely **'locked in'** last night, and I'm ready for this exam.

**Feifei**: Good luck, and we'll be back next time with another useful English phrase. See you soon!

**Beth**: Goodbye.
