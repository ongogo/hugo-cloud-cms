### 中文简介

本期节目讲解高频词 **'note'** 的 **4 个不同含义**（除常见的"便条/笔记"外）：
1. **注意/观察** — *note something*
2. **写下/记录** — *note something down*
3. **音符** — *a musical note*
4. **纸币** — *a note / banknote*

Georgie 通过生动例句快速演示每个用法，适合碎片化学习。

---

## 4 个含义详解

### 1. **notice or observe** — 注意/观察

> **用法**：*note + 宾语*
> 
> **含义**：留意、注意到、观察到、认识到

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Make sure to **note** the unusual wooden features of this house.* | 请务必**注意**这栋房子独特的木质特征。 |
| *I **noted** that your son did all the washing up – you have brought him up well!* | 我**留意到**你儿子把碗都洗了——你教育得真好！ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 多用于正式/半正式语境
- ✅ 意为"留意、将...记在心上"
- ✅ 常用结构：*note that... / note how... / note the...*

---

### 2. **write or record something for the future** — 写下/记录

> **用法**：*note sth (down) / make a note of sth*
> 
> **含义**：把...写下来、记下来，以免忘记

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Thanks for the advice. I'll **note that** for next time.* | 谢谢建议。我**记下来了**，下次用得上。 |
| *Make sure to **note down** the date of the test – you will need to revise.* | 务必**记下**考试日期——你得复习了。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ *note down* 比 *note* 更强调"写在纸上/设备里"
- ✅ 固定搭配：*make a note of... / take notes*
- ✅ 同义：*jot down, write down, record*

---

### 3. **musical sound** — 音符

> **用法**：*a note / notes* (可数名词)
> 
> **含义**：单个乐音、音符（代表音高和时值的符号）

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *This song is hard to sing. It has a lot of high **notes**.* | 这首歌难唱。**高音**太多了。 |
| *I think the composer has written down the wrong **note** here – it sounds like it's clashing with the rest of the music.* | 我觉得作曲家这里**写错了音**——听起来跟其它部分冲突。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 可数名词：*a note, two notes, high/low notes*
- ✅ 相关词汇：*pitch (音高), tone (音调), scale (音阶), chord (和弦)*
- ✅ 口语常说：*hit the right note (唱准音/说到点子上)*

---

### 4. **paper money** — 纸币

> **用法**：*a note / notes* (可数名词)；*banknote* 同义
> 
> **含义**：纸币、钞票（英式英语常用，*美式常用 bill*）

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Sorry, I don't have any small change. Is it OK to pay in **notes**?* | 对不起，我没零钱。**纸币**付款可以吗？ |
| *Hey! There were two £20 **notes** in my wallet when I gave it to you, and now there is only one!* | 嘿！我给你钱包时里面有两张 **20镑钞票**，现在只剩一张了！ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ **英式英语**常用 *note/banknote*，美式常用 *bill*
- ✅ 常用面额：*a £5 note, a $10 bill, a €20 note*
- ✅ 相关词汇：*coin (硬币), change (零钱), currency (货币)*

## 批判性思考题

1. **多义词认知负荷**：同一个词 *note* 作动词(注意/记录)和名词(音符/纸币/便条)各有 4 义。二语学习者如何建立"核心义→派生义"的语义网络，而非死记硬背？

2. **动词语态差异**：含义 1 *note* (注意) 多用主动语态，含义 2 *be noted* (被记录) 常用被动。为什么"被注意到"较少说 *be noted* 而多用 *be noticed*？(*note* 侧重主观认知,*notice* 侧重感知)

3. **英美词汇差异**：含义 4 *note* (英式) vs *bill* (美式)。还有哪些"货币/支付"词汇存在英美差异？(*coin/change, cheque/check, cash/notes*)

4. **隐喻延伸**：*note* 从"标记/记号"→"便条"→"音符(记录音高的记号)"→"纸币(代表价值的记号)"。这种"符号代表功能"的隐喻链条如何解释其他多义词？

5. **短语动词扩展**：*note down, note that, make a note of, take note of, take notes, compare notes, strike the right note*。这些搭配如何体现 *note* 作"认知记录单位"的核心概念？

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Georgie**: You know that this is a note, don't you? But do you know these other meanings? 'To note something' can mean to notice it or observe it.

**Example**: *Make sure to **note** the unusual wooden features of this house.*

**Georgie**: It can also mean to write or record something for the future.

**Example**: *Thanks for the advice. I'll **note that** for next time.*

**Georgie**: A note can also be musical. A musical **note** is a sound in music – laaa! It tells us how high or low a sound is and how long it lasts.

**Example**: *This song is hard to sing. It has a lot of high **notes**.*

**Georgie**: A note is also paper money – these are banknotes. You can say banknote as one word or just a note on its own.

**Example**: *Sorry, I don't have any small change. Is it OK to pay in **notes**?*

**Georgie**: So, a note is paper money, a musical sound and a verb, which means to notice something or write it down. Which meanings of 'note' did you know?
