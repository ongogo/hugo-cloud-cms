### 中文简介

本期节目讲解高频词 **'post'** 的 **4 个不同含义**（除常见的"邮件/邮递"外）：
1. **网络发帖/文章** — *a post / to post*
2. **职位/岗位** — *a post / job post*
3. **立柱/桩** — *a fence post / sign post*
4. **前缀 'post-' 表 '事后/后'** — *post-match / post-war*

Georgie 通过生动例句快速演示每个用法，适合碎片化学习。

---

## 4 个含义详解

### 1. **an online publication** — 网络发帖/文章

> **用法**：*a post* (名词) / *to post* (动词)
> 
> **含义**：在社交媒体、论坛、博客上发布的短消息、文章或媒体内容

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *I saw your **post** about your latest holiday – it looked amazing!* | 我看了你关于最近假期的**帖子/动态**——看起来太棒了！ |
| *Don't forget to **post** that update about the latest fashion trends.* | 别忘了**发布/发帖**关于最新时尚趋势的更新。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 名词：*a post, a social media post, a blog post*
- ✅ 动词：*to post sth, post an update, post a photo*
- ✅ 现代高频用法，社交媒体核心词汇

---

### 2. **a job or position** — 职位/岗位

> **用法**：*a post* / *a job post* (名词)
> 
> **含义**：工作中的一个职位、角色，或招聘广告

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *I saw a **job post** for my dream role. I think I'm going to apply for it.* | 我看到了梦想职位的**招聘帖/职位信息**。我想申请。 |
| *I don't think I'll be able to give you a tour – I've got to stick to my **post** as a doorkeeper.* | 我恐怕不能带你参观——我得坚守我的**岗位/哨位**(守门人)。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 正式/职场语境常用
- ✅ *job post* = 招聘信息，*stick to one's post* = 坚守岗位
- ✅ 同义：*position, role, vacancy, appointment*

---

### 3. **a long piece of wood or metal that stands in the ground** — 立柱/桩

> **用法**：*a post* (可数名词)
> 
> **含义**：固定在地面上的垂直木柱、金属杆(如围栏柱、路标柱)

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *I can't park my car there – there's a **post** in the way!* | 我不能把车停那儿——有根**立柱/桩**挡着路！ |
| *That fence **post** looks like it is rotten – we should replace it.* | 那根**围栏柱**看起来烂了——咱们得换掉。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 实物名词，可数：*a post, fence posts, sign posts*
- ✅ 常见搭配：*fence post, gate post, lamp post, sign post, goal post*
- ✅ 物理支撑结构的核心部件

---

### 4. **a prefix meaning 'after'** — 前缀 "post-" 表 "后/事后"

> **用法**：*post- + 名词/形容词*
> 
> **含义**：表示"在...之后"、"...以后"的时间段

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *When the game is finished, I really enjoy watching the players do their **post-match** interviews.* | 比赛结束后，我特爱看球员们的**赛后**采访。 |
| *I find the **post-war** history of France very interesting.* | 我觉得法国**战后**历史很有意思。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 构词前缀：*post- + 名词 = "事后/后..."*
- ✅ 常见组词：*post-match, post-war, postgraduate, postpartum, postpone, postscript (P.S.)*
- ✅ 反义前缀：*pre- (pre-match, pre-war, prenatal)*

## 批判性思考题

1. **核心义→派生义网络**：*post* 原义"竖立的木桩/标记"→"邮政系统的标记点/站点"→"邮件"→"岗位/职位(固定点)"→"网络发布点(虚拟站点)"→"前缀 post-(标记时间点)"。画出语义演变树状图。

2. **名词/动词零派生**：*post* 作名词"帖子"和动词"发帖"零标记转换。英语中此类"工具/平台名词→使用该工具动词"的零派生还有哪些？(*google, skype, whatsapp, email, text, message, friend, follow, like*)

3. **前缀生产力**：*post-* 作时间前缀极具生产力(*post-pandemic, post-truth, post-colonial, post-industrial, post-human*)。对比 *pre-, mid-, anti-, pro-* 的生产力与语体色彩差异。

4. **多义词歧义消解**：句子 *"He left his post"* 至少有 3 种解读(辞职/离岗/离开木桩)。语境如何消歧？设计歧义消解练习。

5. **数字时代语义扩展**：物理 *post(柱/桩)* → 虚拟 *post(帖子)* 隐喻映射。类似"物理实体→虚拟对应物"的语义扩展在中文里有哪些？(*首页/页面/窗口/桌面/文件夹/垃圾桶/剪贴板*)

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Georgie**: You probably know this kind of post, right? But you might not know these other meanings. 'Post' can mean to publish online – that's a verb. But it can also be an online publication – that's a noun.

**Example**: *I've drafted a social media **post** about my recent trip. Should I **post** it?*

**Georgie**: A **post** can be a job or position, or an advert for that position.

**Example**: *I saw a **job post** for my dream role. I think I'm going to apply for it.*

**Georgie**: A **post** can be something physical. A fence **post** – a long piece of wood that stands in the ground.

**Example**: *I can't park my car there – there's a **post** in the way!*

**Georgie**: Lastly, '**post**' can be used as a prefix meaning 'after'.

**Example**: *When the game's finished, I really enjoy watching the players do their **post-match** interviews.*

**Georgie**: So, '**post**' can be a prefix meaning 'after', a long piece of wood, a job and a social media publication. Which meanings did you know?
