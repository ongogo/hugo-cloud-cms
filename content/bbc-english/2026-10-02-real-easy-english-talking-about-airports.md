## 重点词汇表

| 单词/短语 | 英文释义 | 中文释义 | 来源语境 |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| **check in** | officially tell the plane company that you will get on the plane, often giving them your luggage at the same time | 办理登机手续；托运行李 | 机场第一步 |
| **boarding pass** | ticket used to get on the plane, showing your seat number | 登机牌 | check-in 后拿到 |
| **go through security** | pass through an area where you are checked for anything that you shouldn't carry on a plane | 过安检 | 核心流程 |
| **boarding gate** | area where you wait just before you get on the plane | 登机口 | 等待登机区域 |
| **catch a flight** | successfully get on a plane | 赶上航班 | 成功登机 |
| **miss a flight** | don't get on a plane that you planned to travel on, often because you are too late | 误机；赶不上航班 | 失败情况 |

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 机场核心流程

> **Neil**: *"One of the most important things is **check in**. Now, to **check in** is to officially tell the airline that you're going to fly. Normally, now, we do this online, but you can still **check in** at the airport, I think."*
> 
> **Neil**: *"最重要的事是**办理登机**。就是正式告诉航空公司你要飞。现在通常网上办，但机场柜台也能办。"*

> **Becca**: *"They give you a **boarding pass** then, don't they?"*
> 
> **Becca**: *"然后他们给你**登机牌**，对吧？"*

> **Neil**: *"The **boarding pass** is the document that lets you get on the plane and also tells you what seat that you have."*
> 
> **Neil**: *"**登机牌**是让你上飞机的文件，也告诉你座位号。"*

> **Neil**: *"Then you need to **go through security**, and that's when they check you to make sure you haven't got anything that you shouldn't have on a plane."*
> 
> **Neil**: *"然后**过安检**，检查你有没有带违禁品。"*

> **Becca**: *"After security, you wait at the **boarding gate** and this is the area that you wait at before you get on the plane."*
> 
> **Becca**: *"过完安检，在**登机口**等，那是上飞机前等待的区域。"*

### 2. 关键动作对比：Catch vs Miss

| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **catch a flight** | 成功登机、赶上航班 | *I caught the flight.* (我赶上了航班。) |
| **miss a flight** | 误机、没赶上 | *I missed the flight.* (我误机了。) |

**Neil 的误机经历**：
> *I missed a flight once... travelling in Ireland... flight in Dublin... I was in Cork... missed the train from Cork to Dublin... couldn't get to the airport on time.*

**Becca 的惊险经历**：
> *I was very close to missing a flight... travelled from Vietnam, really tired... wasn't listening carefully... last person to board... embarrassing.*

### 3. 广播叫名的尴尬

> **Neil**: *"If you get your name read out on the loudspeaker in an airport, that's really embarrassing because everyone in the airport is thinking, 'Who's that person? That person who's going to miss their flight!'*
> 
> **Neil**: *机场广播念你名字**超尴尬**，所有人都想："谁啊？那要误机的人！"*

## 批判性思考题

1. **机场流程的数字化演变**：*online check-in, mobile boarding pass, biometric gates, self-bag-drop* 如何重塑 *passenger journey*？*"seamless travel, frictionless airport"* 还面临哪些 *digital divide* (数字鸿沟) 问题？

2. **安检心理学**：*"guilty until proven innocent"* (未证实有罪先感愧) 现象。*security theater* (安检表演) vs *effective screening* (有效筛查) 的平衡。*behavioral detection, profiling, false positives* 如何用英语讨论？

3. **误机的经济与心理成本**：*rebooking fees, accommodation, missed connections, stress, guilt*。*travel insurance* 覆盖范围、*airline duty of care* (照护义务) *EU261/UK261* 赔偿规则。

4. **机场作为"非场所"**：*Marc Augé 的 "non-places" (非场所)*——机场、高速路、超市。*"transience, anonymity, solitude"* 如何在机场极致呈现？*airport sociology*。

5. **跨文化机场词汇**：中文"值机/登机牌/安检/登机口/赶飞机/误机" 对应英文。*boarding pass* 美式也说 *boarding pass*，英式有时用 *boarding card*。*gate* 通用。

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Neil**: Hi Becca.

**Becca**: Hi Neil. How are you? Have you got any trips coming up soon?

**Neil**: I have. I'm going to Montenegro, which is exciting. I've never been there before, but it means that I have to go to an airport and I have to fly. I don't like flying.

**Becca**: But we're talking about airports today in this episode of Real Easy English.

**Neil**: We are. But people need to know that to get a transcript and a worksheet for this episode, they need to go to our website, bbclearningenglish.com.

**Becca**: Absolutely. So, you'll be going to an airport for your holiday, but what happens at an airport, Neil?

**Neil**: One of the most important things is **check in**. Now, to **check in** is to officially tell the airline that you're going to fly. Normally, now, we do this online, but you can still **check in** at the airport, I think.

**Becca**: Yes, you can. Yeah. So, there will be **check-in** gates. It is very important.

**Neil**: It is, because they give you a **boarding pass** then, don't they?

**Becca**: They do.

**Neil**: The **boarding pass** is the document that lets you get on the plane and also tells you what seat that you have on the plane as well.

**Becca**: So, you've checked in and you've got your boarding pass. What happens next?

**Neil**: Well, then you need to **go through security**, and that's when they check you to make sure you haven't got anything that you shouldn't have on a plane.

**Becca**: I hate airport security. Even though I know that I'm not carrying anything that I'm not supposed to have, it still makes me feel a bit stressed.

**Neil**: Yeah, I know what you mean. You feel guilty even if you haven't done anything.

**Becca**: Exactly! Yeah, airport security is one of the reasons I don't really enjoy airports.

**Neil**: Really? You don't like airports?

**Becca**: I don't like airports. I enjoy flying but... yeah, airports can be a bit stressful for me.

**Neil**: Well, I'm the exact opposite of you because I quite like airports, but I really don't like flying.

**Becca**: What do you like about airports?

**Neil**: I think because when you finally get to the airport, it's somebody else's problem for you to travel. So, it's my responsibility to get to the airport but once I'm there, I can relax.

**Becca**: OK. And have you ever been in a situation where you relaxed a bit too much and almost missed a flight?

**Neil**: Yes, I have **missed a flight** once and it was very stressful. I was travelling in Ireland and I had a flight in Dublin, and I was in a different city – in Cork. And I **missed the train** from Cork to Dublin and so I couldn't get to the airport on time.

**Becca**: So, you did **miss that flight**?

**Neil**: I **missed it**.

**Becca**: Oh no!

**Neil**: So, Becca, have you ever **missed a flight**?

**Becca**: Thankfully, no, I haven't **missed a flight**. I was very close to **missing a flight**... I was just relaxing. I had travelled from Vietnam and I was really tired, so I wasn't really listening carefully. Well, they didn't call my name, so I was really stressed trying to find the gate, but luckily I **caught it**. I think I was the last person to board the plane, and that's always a little bit embarrassing as well.

**Neil**: You know what, Becca? I think if you get your name read out on the loudspeaker in an airport, that's really embarrassing because everyone in the airport is thinking, "Who's that person? That person who's going to **miss their flight**!"

**Becca**: And you don't want to be that person.

**Neil**: OK. Becca, it's time to recap the vocabulary that we used in this episode, starting with **check in**. **Check in** is the verb, which means you officially tell the plane company that you plan to get on the plane, and you give them your bags as well. And at the airport you do this at the **check-in desk**.

**Becca**: Yes. And at the **check-in desk**, that's where you get your **boarding pass**, and that's the ticket that you need to get on the plane.

**Neil**: And then we heard the expression **go through security**. If you **go through security**, you go through that area where they check and make sure you have nothing with you that should not be on a plane.

**Becca**: Yes. Going through security – my least favourite thing about airports. After security, you wait at the **boarding gate** and this is the area that you wait at before you get on the plane.

**Neil**: If you **catch a flight**, it means you successfully get on the plane and fly somewhere, but if you **miss a flight**, like I did in Ireland, it means that you were too late to get on the plane.

**Becca**: Well, Neil, that's it for this week's episode of Real Easy English, but we'll be back next week with another episode.

**Neil**: Don't forget that you can download a free worksheet on our website, bbclearningenglish.com. Goodbye for now.

**Becca**: Bye!
