## 详细词汇解析

### 1. **take to the streets** (动词短语)

> **定义**：*gather in a public place to oppose or celebrate something*

> **中文**：走上街头；集会示威；游行

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **抗议示威** | *Thousands **took to the streets** to demand a change in government.* | 成千上万的人**走上街头**，要求更换政府。 |
| **庆祝活动** | *Fans **took to the streets** after the team won the championship.* | 球队夺冠后，球迷们**涌上街头庆祝**。 |
| **社会运动** | *Activists **take to the streets** every Saturday to protest climate inaction.* | 活动人士每周六**走上街头**，抗议气候不作为。 |

**同义表达**：*take to the road, march, demonstrate, protest, rally*

### 2. **cost of living** (名词短语)

> **定义**：*amount of money needed to pay for everyday essentials like food and energy bills*

> **中文**：生活成本；生活开销；物价水平

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **经济压力** | *The rising **cost of living** is putting pressure on families.* | 不断上涨的**生活成本**给家庭带来压力。 |
| **住房负担** | *Young people can't afford rent because of the high **cost of living**.* | 由于**生活成本**高昂，年轻人负担不起房租。 |
| **薪资讨论** | *Workers are demanding higher wages to keep up with the **cost of living**.* | 工人要求提高工资，以跟上**生活成本**。 |

**同义表达**：*living expenses, cost of life, household expenses*

**搭配**：
- *a high cost of living* (高生活成本)
- *the rising cost of living* (不断上涨的生活成本)
- *cost of living crisis* (生活成本危机)

### 3. **stacked against** (短语，习语用法)

> **定义**：*when an unfair or difficult situation makes it harder for someone to succeed*

> **中文**：不利于……；处于劣势；牌面不利；形势严峻

| 语境 | 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|------|
| **体育比赛** | *The football team had a few injured players. The odds were **stacked against** them.* | 足球队有几名球员受伤，**形势对他们不利**。 |
| **求职竞争** | *Without a university degree, the odds are **stacked against** young job seekers.* | 没有大学学位，年轻求职者**处于劣势**。 |
| **法律诉讼** | *The defendant felt the legal system was **stacked against** him.* | 被告觉得法律制度**对他不利**。 |

**同义表达**：*unfavorable, against the odds, uphill battle, rigged*

## 批判性思考题

1. **“无领袖”运动的组织**：许多青年抗议在线上组织且没有明确领袖，这种去中心化结构如何影响运动的持续性与效果？*decentralized organizing, leaderless movement, social media mobilization*
2. **“生活成本”与抗议动因**：*cost of living* 上涨如何转化为街头抗议？经济压力与政治诉求之间有何联系？*economic hardship, cost of living crisis, political participation*
3. **“形势不利”的框架**：*stacked against* 暗示系统性不公平。年轻人面临哪些结构性障碍（教育、住房、就业）？*systemic inequality, structural barriers, intergenerational justice*
4. **跨国抗议的相似性**：世界各地青年抗议在线上相互模仿，这种“抗议扩散”现象背后的机制是什么？*protest diffusion, transnational activism, digital activism*
5. **政府投资不足**：Naya Prakash 指出政府对青年服务和政策投资不足。增加投资是否能减少抗议？*public investment, youth policy, government responsiveness*
