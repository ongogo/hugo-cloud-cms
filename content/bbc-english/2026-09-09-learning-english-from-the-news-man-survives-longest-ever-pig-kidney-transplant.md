## 新闻故事梗概

### The Story

Tim Andrews from New Hampshire in the United States was aged 66 when he found out that he’d developed kidney disease linked to type 2 diabetes.

At that time, he was at the end-stage of the disease, meaning that he was in the final, most severe phase. Not much can be done at this stage, and many people in this situation are waiting for a replacement human kidney. There is shortage of human donor kidneys.

Andrews received a pig kidney, and it worked inside him for 271 days, the longest on record, US doctors say. However, the pig organ eventually failed and was removed so Andrews needed some dialysis, a medical treatment where a machine functions like a kidney.

He then received a human kidney transplant 82 days later.

## 核心词汇详解

### 1. **transplant** — 移植；调动；迁移

> **含义**：move or be moved from one place to another（从一个地方移动到另一个地方）

> **例句**：
> *The donor heart was **transplanted** in a seven-hour operation.*
>
> 捐献者的心脏在一场七小时的手术中被**移植**。

**关键点**：
- ✅ 可作动词（to transplant sth）或名词（a transplant）
- ✅ 常用于医疗语境：organ transplant（器官移植）、kidney transplant（肾移植）
- ✅ 也可指非医疗的迁移/调动

---

### 2. **first-of-its-kind** — 史无前例的；首创的

> **含义**：completely original and has never existed or happened before（完全原创，从未存在或发生过）

> **例句**：
> *The city launched a **first-of-its-kind** electric bus network that runs entirely on solar power.*
>
> 该市推出了一套完全由太阳能驱动的**史无前例**的电动公交车网络。

**关键点**：
- ✅ 形容词短语，通常用连字符连接
- ✅ 强调“首创”“从未有过”
- ✅ 常搭配：*a first-of-its-kind + 名词*（product, treatment, network, study…）

---

### 3. **bridge** — 桥梁；过渡；临时方案

> **含义**：a temporary solution that helps someone get from one stage to another（帮助某人从一个阶段过渡到另一个阶段的临时方案）

> **例句**：
> *Her internship acted as a **bridge** between university and her dream career.*
>
> 她的实习在大学与梦想职业之间充当了**桥梁**。

**关键点**：
- ✅ 原意“桥梁”，引申为“过渡手段”
- ✅ 常用搭配：*act as a bridge / serve as a bridge / a bridge between A and B*
- ✅ 在本新闻中，猪肾是人肾移植前的“桥梁”
