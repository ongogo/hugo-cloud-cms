| 单词/短语 | 英文释义 | 中文释义 | 来源语境 |

| **itch / itchy** | unpleasant feeling on your skin / feeling something unpleasant on your skin | 痒/痒的 | 核心话题 |
| **scratch** | rub your nails back and forth against something, often to relieve an itch | 抓挠；搔 | 动作 |

| **catch (an illness/feeling)** | get an illness or feeling from someone else | 传染；被传染 | 痒的传染性 |

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心问题：Why do we itch? (为什么会痒？)

> **Neil**: *"An itch is that unpleasant feeling you get on your skin that you have to scratch with your fingers."*
> 
> **Neil**: *"痒是皮肤上那种不舒服的感觉，让你非得用手指抓挠不可。"*

---

### 2. 进化视角：痒是防御机制

> **Sonja Ständer**: *"By scratching the skin, we remove parasites or plants or anything that does not belong on the skin. And so, we start to scratch at the very same area where we itch and remove it. And it can no longer damage the skin and harm ourselves."*
> 
> **Sonja Ständer**: *"抓挠皮肤能清除**寄生虫**或植物或任何不属于皮肤的东西。我们在痒的同一区域抓挠并清除它，这样它就无法再损害皮肤、伤害我们了。"*

**关键洞察**：
- ✅ 痒是**进化保留的保护机制**
- ✅ 抓挠 = 清除 **parasites (寄生虫)**、植物、异物
- ✅ 痒的位置 = 需要清除的位置

---

### 3. 痒的传染性：心理学研究

> **Dr Henning Holle**: *"And what we found in this study was, yes, these scratching videos indeed made people feel itchier. They also started to scratch themselves more spontaneously. So, the answer is basically yes, you can catch a scratch."*
> 
> **Henning Holle 博士**: *"研究发现：看抓挠视频**确实让人更痒**。人们也开始**自发地**更多抓挠。所以答案是肯定的，**你能'传染'到抓挠**。"*

> **Georgie**: *"Catch in this context means get from someone else."*
> 
> **Georgie**: *这里的 *catch* 意思是**从别人那里获得/被传染**。*

**关键发现**：
| 现象 | 解释 |
|------|------|
| **传染性痒** | 看别人抓挠 → 自己也痒 |
| **自发抓挠** | *spontaneously* = 无意识、不假思索 |
| **社会传染** | 类似 *catch a cold* (感冒)、*catch a yawn* (打哈欠传染) |

---

### 4. 测验答案揭晓

> **Sonja Ständer**: *"It sounds like hairy skin so I would go for the scalp."*
> 
> **Anand Jagatia**: *"Yeah, I think it's beard or something because it does sound like there's hair there."*
> 
> **Dr Henning Holle**: *"It is definitely hairy skin and Anand was spot on. So, it is indeed a **beard scratch** that we were listening to."*
> 
> **Henning Holle 博士**: *"绝对是毛发生皮肤，Anand 说中了。确实是**刮胡子/抓胡子**的声音。"*

## 批判性思考题

1. **进化心理学视角**：痒作为 *"adaptive defense mechanism"* (适应性防御机制)，如何解释 *"phantom itch" (幻肢痒/无刺激痒)*、*"psychogenic pruritus" (心因性瘙痒)*？是 *"false alarm" (误报)* 还是 *"evolutionary mismatch" (进化错配)*？

2. **社会传染的神经基础**：*"contagious itch" (传染性痒)* 与 *"contagious yawning" (传染性打哈欠)*、*"emotional contagion" (情绪传染)* 共享 *"mirror neuron system" (镜像神经元系统)*？fMRI 研究显示什么脑区激活？(*insula, somatosensory cortex*)

3. **Scratch-Itch Cycle 的恶性循环**：*"itch-scratch cycle" (瘙痒-抓挠循环)* 中，*scratching → histamine release → more itch*。慢性瘙痒患者如何用 *"habit reversal training" (习惯逆转训练)* 打破循环？

4. **跨模态感官传染**：*audio-only scratching sounds* 即可诱发痒感，说明 *"cross-modal sensory integration" (跨模态感觉整合)*。视觉、触觉、听觉哪个通道传染性最强？

5. **语言中的 Catch 隐喻**：*catch a cold, catch a yawn, catch a scratch, catch feelings, catch the vibe*。*"catch"* 从物理接触 → 抽象传播，体现 *"conceptual metaphor: ACQUIRING IS CATCHING"*。
