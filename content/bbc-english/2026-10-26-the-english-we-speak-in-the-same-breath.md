### 中文简介

本期节目介绍表达 **"in the same breath"**。当你用 "in the same breath" 表达两个想法时，就是对比这两者，并给后一个想法更大的分量。这个表达常与表示转折的连词（如 *but*, *however*, *though*）连用。

> 💡 **核心含义**：used to compare different ideas; it introduces a contradiction. Though different, both ideas could be true, but the latter idea, which comes after 'in the same breath', usually carries more weight.

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达用法

> **Becca**: *"'in the same breath' is used to compare different ideas. It introduces a contradiction. Though different, both ideas could be true, but the latter idea, which comes after 'in the same breath', usually carries more weight."*

> **Becca**: *“in the same breath” 用来比较不同的想法。它引入一种矛盾。虽然两种想法都可能成立，但“in the same breath”之后的那个想法通常分量更重。*

**关键点**：
- ✅ 用于**对比两种看似矛盾但可能同时成立的想法**
- ✅ 后一个想法（in the same breath 之后）通常**更重要、分量更重**
- ✅ 常与 *but, however, though* 等转折连词连用

### 2. 语法重点：转折连词 + in the same breath

> **Feifei**: *"And you used the coordinating conjunction 'but' before the expression."*

> **Becca**: *"Conjunctions that show contrast, such as 'but', 'however' and 'though', can be used before 'in the same breath' to introduce the differences between the ideas."*

常见搭配：
- *but in the same breath*
- *however, in the same breath*
- *though, in the same breath*

## 课文实例解析

### Example 1: 讨厌工作却自愿加班
> *My friend says she hates her job, but then in the same breath, she’s just volunteered for more overtime.*

我的朋友说她讨厌自己的工作，**但与此同时**，她却自愿承担更多加班。 *(后半句分量更重：其实她还愿意工作)*

### Example 2: 答应帮忙却说没时间
> *You said you wanted to help me, but then in the same breath, you say you’ve got no time, you can’t do it. I mean, which is it?*

你说你想帮我，**但与此同时**，你说你没时间，做不到了。我是说，到底怎么样？

### Example 3: 没钱却订昂贵假期
> *My friend says she’s run out of money, but in the same breath, she just told me that she’s booked a really expensive holiday. What’s going on?*

我的朋友说她没钱了，**但与此同时**，她刚刚告诉我说她订了个非常昂贵的假期。这是怎么回事？
