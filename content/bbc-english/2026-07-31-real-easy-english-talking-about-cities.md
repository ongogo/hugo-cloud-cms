## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心词汇用法

> **"London is a huge city – very busy and bustling."**
> 
> 伦敦是个**巨大的城市——非常繁忙热闹**。

> **"It's convenient. There's lots of public transport."**
> 
> **很方便。有很多公共交通。**

> **"I'd like to go to Tbilisi in Georgia."**
> 
> **我想去格鲁吉亚的第比利斯。**

**关键点**：
- ✅ **bustling** = 形容词，修饰城市/街道/市场，褒义
- ✅ **convenient** = 形容词，常用 *It's convenient to...* / *convenient for...*
- ✅ **would like to** = 表达愿望/计划，比 *want to* 更礼貌
- ✅ 级别：A2（初级偏中），语速慢、句子短、重复率高

### 2. 城市优缺点对比

| 优点 | 缺点 |
|------|------|
| **convenient**（方便） | **noisy**（吵） |
| **public transport**（公共交通） | **dirty**（脏） |
| **things to do**（娱乐多） | **busy**（忙/拥挤） |
| **theatres, music, art**（文化丰富） | **expensive**（贵） |
| **spontaneous plans**（随时有事可做） | **pay for everything**（样样花钱） |

## 批判性思考题

1. **城市选择**：如果让你在"便利但贵/吵"的大城市和"安静便宜但不便"的小城镇中选，你会怎么权衡？用英语表达你的理由。

2. **词汇扩展**：*bustling* 的近义词有哪些？(*lively, vibrant, hectic, teeming*) 反义词？(*quiet, sleepy, deserted*)

3. **语法练习**：用 *would like to* 写 3 个你想去的城市和理由。例如：*I'd like to visit Kyoto because I love temples and gardens.*

4. **文化对比**：中文"繁华""便利""宜居"对应哪些英文表达？*bustling/convenient/livable* 还有哪些搭配？

5. **可持续性**：大城市面临拥堵、污染、住房危机。有哪些英语词汇讨论"智慧城市/绿色城市"？(*sustainable, green spaces, public transit, urban planning*)

### Transcript

**Neil**: Hello, I'm Neil.

**Georgie**: And I'm Georgie. Welcome to Real Easy English.

**Neil**: You can find a worksheet and transcript for this episode on our website, bbclearningenglish.com

**Georgie**: Hello Neil. How are you doing?

**Neil**: I'm very well, thank you, Georgie. How are you?

**Georgie**: Yes, I'm very well, thank you. Neil, today we're talking about cities. We both live and work in London, which is a very big city.

**Neil**: It is. It's a huge city – very busy and **bustling**. **Bustling** means there's lots of activity, lots of things going on, busy.

**Georgie**: Speaking of, what do you like about living in a big, **bustling** city?

**Neil**: Well, there are lots of good things about living in a big city. It's **convenient**. It's very **convenient**. So, there is lots of public transport, which is sometimes good. And you can get things that you need quite easily.

**Georgie**: That's very true.

**Neil**: Yeah.

**Georgie**: Yeah. There's also a lot of things to do. We are lucky in London to have lots of theatres where you can see plays. There's a really good music scene, so you can watch and listen to a lot of live music. If you have no plans on a day, you can always find plans, spontaneous plans, so we are really lucky in that sense.

**Neil**: Yeah, and if you like art, there's all kinds of exhibitions and galleries and museums.

**Georgie**: Lots of good things. But there are obviously some bad things about living in a city, aren't there?

**Neil**: Yes.

**Georgie**: Do you want to tell us some?

**Neil**: Yeah. Well, it's very noisy and dirty...

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: ...and busy and expensive.

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: It's always more expensive in a big city, especially the capital city. So, you can do all of the things that we just talked about...

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: ...but you have to pay for them.

**Georgie**: Yeah!

**Neil**: And things like eating out, especially going for a drink, is a really expensive thing to do in the capital city.

**Georgie**: I feel like every time I leave the house I just spend loads of money and that is quite frustrating, but you have to pay for good... for good times, I guess.

**Neil**: Yeah. Have you ever lived in another city apart from London?

**Georgie**: Yes, I have. I used to live in Barcelona, which is quite different to London because, well, it has a beach and the sea on one side and on the other side it has mountains, so it's **contained by nature** and you can get to the nature quite quickly, which is really nice. What about you? Have you lived anywhere different?

**Neil**: Yeah, I've lived in a few big cities – a few capital cities, actually. I've also lived in Spain, but in Madrid. I lived and worked in Prague and that is a really pretty... it's a beautiful city.

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: The architecture looks fantastic.

**Georgie**: Nice buildings. Talking about nice buildings, I lived in Edinburgh because I did my master's degree there and that has beautiful buildings. I loved that city. It has a castle on a big rock in the middle and it's just amazing to look at. So, we've talked about cities we've been to. What about the future? Are there any cities you really want to visit or maybe even live in?

**Neil**: Oh, that's an interesting question! I've never been to Georgia or Armenia or that part of the world, so I'm going to say **I'd like to go to Tbilisi** in Georgia.

**Georgie**: That's a good answer. **I'd love to go there** as well. For me, I'm desperate to go to Latin America. I really want to go to **Rio de Janeiro**, and at the time when the carnival is there, because that sounds really fun and I like to party! Let's recap the vocabulary we heard during the conversation, starting with **bustling**, which means busy with lots of people and activity.

**Neil**: We also heard **convenient**. If something is **convenient**, it means it's very easy to do it. For example, there's lots of public transport in London, so it's **convenient** for travel.

**Georgie**: And we talked about possible plans for the future, using **would like to**.

**Neil**: For example, **I'd like to visit Tbilisi** in Georgia.

**Georgie**: That's it for this episode of Real Easy English, but we'll be back next week with another conversation in easy English.

**Neil**: And you should check out our series The London to Edinburgh Challenge. We've been talking about Edinburgh today. It's a beautiful place and you can see it in this series. It's on our website, bbclearningenglish.com.

**Georgie**: Goodbye for now.

**Neil**: Goodbye!
