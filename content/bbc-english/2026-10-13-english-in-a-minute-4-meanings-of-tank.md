## 四大含义详解

### 1. amount of fuel a car can hold — 汽车油箱容量

> **A tank of fuel** is the maximum amount your car can hold, so can be used to measure what volume of fuel you will need for a journey.

**例句**:
- It's a long drive – it'll take two **tanks**.
  - 路程很远 —— 要跑两箱油。
- We used nearly a full **tank** of petrol getting to Cornwall.
  - 我们去康沃尔差不多用了一整箱汽油。

---

### 2. sleeveless top — 无袖背心

> **A tank top** is a piece of clothing that covers your upper body but has no sleeves.

**例句**:
- Are you trying to show off your muscles with that ridiculous **tank top**?
  - 你穿那件荒唐的背心是想秀肌肉吗？
- It's really hot today, so I'm going to wear a **tank top**.
  - 今天真热，我要穿件无袖背心。

---

### 3. perform very badly — 表现惨败 / 价值暴跌

> If something **tanks**, it performs very badly or loses a lot of value.

**例句**:
- That wasn't the right product. Our profits have **tanked** since we launched it.
  - 那产品不对路。自从上市以来我们的利润**暴跌**了。
- The movie **tanked** after it received so many bad reviews.
  - 那电影收获一堆差评后**票房惨败**。

---

### 4. deliberately lose a game — 故意输掉比赛 / 打假球

> If a team **tanks** a game or match, it deliberately loses.

**例句**:
- What are you doing? Are you trying to **tank** the match?
  - 你干嘛呢？你是想**故意输球**吗？
- She scored an own goal – if that's not **tanking** the game, then I don't know what is!
  - 她乌龙球 —— 这要不是**打假球**，我不知道什么是！
