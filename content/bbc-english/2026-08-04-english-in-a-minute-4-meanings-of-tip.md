### 中文简介

本期节目讲解高频词 **'tip'** 的 **4 个不同含义**（除常见的"建议"外）：
1. **预测某人会成功** — *be tipped to do well*
2. **小费** — *extra money for service*
3. **倾斜/倒出** — *tip something over/into/out of*
4. **垃圾场/废物处理站** — *the tip (place for rubbish)*

Phil 通过生动例句快速演示每个用法，适合碎片化学习。

---

## 4 个含义详解

### 1. **predict to do well** — 预测某人会成功

> **用法**：*be tipped to + 动词* (通常用被动语态)
> 
> **含义**：被预测将会取得成功/获胜

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *She's being **tipped to win** a gold medal this year.* | 她**被看好/被预测将**今年赢得金牌。 |
| *My local MP is **tipped to become** the next prime minister.* | 我选区的议员**被看好将**成为下一任首相。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 多用于体育、政治、娱乐等竞争性领域
- ✅ 被动语态：*be tipped to do sth*
- ✅ 同义：*be favoured to, be predicted to, be tipped for success*

---

### 2. **extra money given to staff for good service** — 小费

> **用法**：*a tip* (名词) / *tip sb* (动词)
> 
> **含义**：给服务人员的额外报酬，表示感谢

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Thank you so much. I've left a **20% tip**.* | 太谢谢了。我留了 **20% 的小费**。 |
| *I've heard if you **tip the staff** at this deli, they'll put extra fillings in your sandwich!* | 听说如果你**给这家熟食店员工小费**，他们会在三明治里多加馅料！ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ 常用于餐厅、酒店、出租车、理发店等服务业
- ✅ 百分比表达：*a 10% tip, a 15% tip, a 20% tip*
- ✅ 动词用法：*tip the waiter, tip the delivery driver*

---

### 3. **move one side down** — 倾斜/倒出/倾倒

> **用法**：*tip sth over / into / out of*
> 
> **含义**：使某物倾斜倒下，或将内容物倒入/倒出容器

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *Weigh the flour and then **tip it into** your mixing bowl.* | 称好面粉，然后**倒进**搅拌碗里。 |
| *I slipped and accidentally **tipped an entire tray of drinks onto** my friend!* | 我滑倒了，**不小心把整托盘饮料泼在了**朋友身上！ |

**关键点**：
- ✅ *tip over* = 打翻、侧翻
- ✅ *tip into* = 倒入
- ✅ *tip out of* = 倒出
- ✅ 物理动作，常用于烹饪、倒液体、搬运场景

---

### 4. **place where rubbish gets taken** — 垃圾场/废物处理站

> **用法**：*the tip* (名词，英式英语)
> 
> **含义**：专门丢弃垃圾/废弃物的场所（美式常说 *dump, landfill, recycling center*）

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| *This garage is full of junk. I need to take it all to **the tip**.* | 车库全是破烂。我得全拿去**垃圾场/废物站**。 |
| *This old microwave doesn't work any more – we will have to take it to **the tip** and see if they can recycle it there.* | 这微波炉坏了——得送去**废物回收站**看能不能回收。 |

**关键点**：
- ✅ **英式英语**常用，美式对应 *dump / landfill / recycling center / transfer station*
- ✅ 固定搭配：*take sth to the tip, drive to the tip, tip fees*
- ✅ 口语化，生活高频

## 批判性思考题

1. **一词多义认知**：同一个单词 *tip* 如何从"尖端/顶端"(名词原义)衍生出"小费""倾斜""预测""垃圾场"四大义项？梳理其隐喻链条。

2. **英美差异**：含义 4 *the tip* 为英式用法，美式对应 *dump/landfill/recycling center*。还有哪些"垃圾处理"相关词汇存在英美差异？(*bin vs trash can, rubbish vs garbage/trash, dustbin vs garbage bin*)

3. **被动语态偏好**：含义 1 *be tipped to* 为何强制被动？对比 *tip sb to win* (主动) 与 *be tipped to win* (被动) 的语用差异。

4. **搭配固定性**：*tip over / into / out of* 体现了英语 phrasal verb 的方向性逻辑。试列举 5 个类似 *pour/empty/spill/dump/transfer* 的动词及其方向介词搭配。

5. **文化小费观**：各国小费文化差异大（美国 15-20% 必给，日本视为失礼，欧洲含服务费）。如何用英语表达"这国家不给小费" / "小费已含在账单里"？

### Transcript

**Phil**: How well do you know the word 'tip'? I know you know that it's a piece of advice, but what about the other meanings? We can use the verb 'tip' to mean 'predict to do well'.

**Example**: *She's being tipped to win a gold medal this year.*

**Phil**: A tip is the extra money you leave for someone who serves you at a restaurant.

**Example**: *Thank you so much. I've left a 20% tip.*

**Phil**: It's also when you move something like this. You can tip something into or out of something else, or you can you just tip it over so that it falls onto the floor.

**Example**: *Weigh the flour and then tip it into your mixing bowl.*

**Phil**: 'Tip' as a noun also refers to the place where rubbish gets taken.

**Example**: *This garage is full of junk. I need to take it all to the tip.*
