### 中文简介

本期节目探讨**情绪低落、脾气不好**时的表达。Neil 和 Georgie 用简单英语聊聊什么让人 **"grumpy"**（饿、累、热导致睡不好、月经期、被困室内工作），以及如何 **"cheer yourself up"**（吃东西、睡觉、运动、散步、听音乐跳舞）。学习核心词汇：**grumpy、in a bad mood、in a good mood、cheerful、cheer up**。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心词汇用法

> **"I was really grumpy this morning."**
> 
> 我今天早上**心情很不好/很暴躁**。

> **"I get really grumpy if I'm hungry or tired."**
> 
> 我要是**饿了或累了就会很暴躁**。

**关键点**：
- ✅ **grumpy** = 形容词，形容人易怒、心情不好、爱发脾气
- ✅ 常用结构：**get grumpy if...** / **feel grumpy when...**
- ✅ 程度副词搭配：**really grumpy, very grumpy, a bit grumpy**

### 2. 心情对比：Good vs Bad Mood

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **in a bad mood** | 心情不好 | *Neil was in a bad mood this morning.* |
| **in a good mood** | 心情好 | *Georgie has been in a good mood all day.* |
| **grumpy** | 易怒、不高兴 | *He's grumpy when he's hungry.* |
| **cheerful** | 兴高采烈、显而易见的快乐 | *She's always so cheerful.* |

### 3. 动词短语：cheer up

> **"What do you do to cheer yourself up?"**
> 
> 你做什么让**自己开心起来**？

> **"Exercise is a really good thing to lift your mood."**
> 
> 运动是**提升心情**的好办法。

**关键点**：
- ✅ **cheer up** (不及物) = 变开心：*Cheer up! It's not that bad.*
- ✅ **cheer sb up** (及物) = 让某人开心：*The movie cheered me up.*
- ✅ **cheer yourself up** = 让自己开心：*I cheer myself up by dancing.*
- ✅ **lift your mood** = 提升心情（稍正式）

### 4. 让人 grumpy 的原因 vs 让人 cheer up 的方法

| 让人 grumpy 的原因 | 让人 cheer up 的方法 |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| **hungry** (饿) | **eat something** (吃点东西) |
| **tired** (累) | **sleep** (睡觉) |
| **hot / difficult to sleep** (热/睡不好) | **exercise** (运动) |
| **no food in fridge** (冰箱没吃的) | **go for a walk in the park** (去公园散步) |
| **certain times of the month** (生理期) | **be outside in nature** (亲近大自然) |
| **stuck inside on sunny day** (好天被困室内) | **fresh air** (呼吸新鲜空气) |
| | **upbeat music + dance** (欢快音乐跳舞) |
| | **coffee** (咖啡) |

## 批判性思考题

1. **情绪词汇辨析**：*grumpy, irritable, moody, cranky, in a bad mood* 都表示"心情不好"，但在语体色彩、程度、持续时间上有何细微差别？

2. **生理与情绪关联**：Neil 说 *hungry and tired → very grumpy*。这种"生理需求未满足导致情绪失控"在心理学上叫 *hangry (hungry + angry)*。你有类似体验吗？如何用英语表达？

3. **cheer up 的自反性**：*cheer yourself up* 强调主动调节情绪。对比 *wait for it to pass* (等过去) vs *actively cheer up*。哪种策略更有效？有文化差异吗？

4. **隐藏情绪 vs 表达情绪**：Georgie 说 *good at hiding it* 但也认为 *good to share how you feel*。职场/社交中，何时该隐藏 grumpy，何时该表达？英语中如何委婉表达"I'm not in a good mood"？

4. **文化对比**：中文"起床气""饿急眼""心情不好""不爽""暴躁"对应 *grumpy* 的不同程度。如何根据语境选择最贴切的中文对应词？

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Neil**: Hi Georgie, how are you today?

**Georgie**: I'm pretty good today, thank you, Neil. How are you?

**Neil**: I am much better than I was this morning.

**Georgie**: Oh. How were you feeling this morning?

**Neil**: I was really **grumpy**.

**Georgie**: Oh! Why is that?

**Neil**: Well, I'll tell you in a minute, but that's what we're talking about today – being **grumpy**, which means being **in a bad mood**.

**Georgie**: Yes, but before we begin, our listeners need to know that they can find a worksheet, a free worksheet, on our website at bbclearningenglish.com. So, you said you were feeling **grumpy** this morning. I didn't notice that. Why were you feeling **grumpy** this morning?

**Neil**: I get really **grumpy** if I'm hungry or tired, and very, very **grumpy** if I'm hungry and tired.

**Georgie**: Oh no! Is that how you were feeling this morning then?

**Neil**: Yeah. It's very hot at the moment – difficult to sleep.

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: And we also didn't have any food in the fridge, so I came to work hungry and tired, and so I was grumpy.

**Georgie**: Oh well, you hid it very well. I didn't notice that. I've felt **cheerful** all day so far.

**Neil**: Well, you're always **cheerful**, Georgie. Or are you?

**Georgie**: I... No, I'm not always **cheerful**. Mostly, I have to say, mostly I am. But there are certain times of the month that make me feel very **grumpy**. And when I'm **in a bad mood**, I become a bit **irritable**. Small things that other people do annoy me. And sometimes it's not for any reason or it's for a very small reason and I shouldn't be so grumpy.

**Neil**: OK. Georgie, what kind of things make you grumpy?

**Georgie**: Well, as I said, there are certain times of the month where I feel grumpy, but other things that make me grumpy... Usually, if it's a really sunny day outside and I'm stuck inside working, that makes me feel a bit grumpy because I would prefer to be outside in the sun, in the park, spending time in the fresh air, but instead I'm working. So, you said that you're grumpy when you're hungry or tired. What kind of things do you do to **cheer yourself up**?

**Neil**: Ah yeah, to **cheer myself up** – to feel better. Well, eat something and sleep. But I guess also do something relaxing. Maybe do some exercise. Exercise is a really good thing to **lift your mood**.

**Georgie**: Yes. I, as I said, like to be outside. That will make me... That will put me **in a better mood**. Going for a walk in the park, being in nature with some fresh air. I also really like to put some **upbeat music** on and **dance around my bedroom**. I like doing that. That **cheers me up**.

**Neil**: OK. I think that would cheer everybody up.

**Georgie**: What? If they got to watch it?

**Neil**: Yeah!

**Georgie**: What do you think about people who are always grumpy?

**Neil**: I do know someone who's always grumpy, but he's so grumpy, it's almost funny.

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: Like, he knows he's grumpy. And people make little jokes about it and he, kind of, knows he's grumpy.

**Georgie**: Mmm. So, it's entertaining for you.

**Neil**: Yeah. But it's still not really that nice to be close to.

**Georgie**: Yeah. A bit of a negative energy.

**Neil**: Absolutely. Georgie are you good at hiding it when you are grumpy.

**Georgie**: Well, apparently I am, because I sometimes am grumpy and you said that I never seem grumpy, so maybe I am very good at hiding it. I think it's good not to let other people feel your negativity coming out of you, but on the other hand I think it's also good to share how you feel sometimes.

**Neil**: OK. So, perhaps you are really grumpy right now...

**Georgie**: Yeah.

**Neil**: ...even though you seem cheerful.

**Georgie**: Even though I have a big smile on my face, yeah. It's because I've had a coffee. I'm feeling good.

**Neil**: OK. Georgie, it's time now to recap the language that we've used in this podcast.

**Georgie**: Yes, we had **grumpy**, which means easily annoyed, negative or **in a bad mood**.

**Neil**: Yes, **in a bad mood**. And the opposite of that is **in a good mood**, when you're feeling happy and **cheerful**. **Cheerful** is another word we heard today.

**Georgie**: Yes, **cheerful** is another word for happy, but you show it in an obvious way, maybe by smiling.

**Neil**: Yep. And we've got the phrasal verb **cheer up**, which means to become happy or cheerful.

**Georgie**: Yes. For example, I **cheer myself up** by dancing alone in my bedroom.

**Neil**: That's it for this episode of Real Easy English but come back next week for another episode.

**Georgie**: Yes. And don't forget, you can find a free transcript and worksheet to practise what you've learnt on our website, bbclearningenglish.com. Goodbye for now.

**Neil**: Goodbye.
