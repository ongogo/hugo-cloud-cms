### 中文简介

本期节目介绍表达 **"turn over a new leaf"** 的用法。意思是**"翻开新的一页/改过自新/重新开始"**，指停止消极行为、做出积极改变。Feifei 和 Phil 通过新年计划、咬指甲、小时候调皮、依赖手机等例子展示用法，并介绍同义表达 **"turn the page"**。

## 课文重点解析

### 1. 核心表达含义与词源

> **Feifei**: *"The 'leaf' in this expression is a leaf of paper, like a sheet of paper, so to 'turn over a new leaf' means to turn the page and start a new chapter of your story."*
> 
> **Feifei**: *"这个表达里的 'leaf' 是指**书页/纸张**，所以 'turn over a new leaf' 意思是**翻过一页，开启人生新章节**。"*

**关键点**：
- ✅ **leaf** = 书页，非树叶
- ✅ **隐喻**：人生如书，翻页 = 新开始
- ✅ **核心义**：停止消极行为，积极改变
- ✅ **常用时机**：新年、生日、新工作、分手后、任何想改变时

---

### 2. 与 New Year's Resolution 的关系

> **Phil**: *"So, it's a bit like making a new year's resolution?"*
> 
> **Feifei**: *"Yes."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"那有点像**新年计划**？"*
> 
> **Feifei**: *"对。"*

| 维度 | **Turn over a new leaf** | **New Year's Resolution** |
|------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| **时间** | 任何时候 | 通常新年 |
| **语气** | 行动导向、决心强 | 计划性、愿望性 |
| **范围** | 具体行为改变 | 可宽泛目标 |
| **隐喻** | 翻书页 | 立誓/许愿 |

---

### 3. 同义表达：Turn the page

> **Phil**: *"A similar expression that people use is 'turn the page'. We can use it metaphorically to talk about starting a new chapter in our lives."*
> 
> **Phil**: *"类似表达是 'turn the page'。我们可用它隐喻**开启人生新篇章**。"*

> **Feifei**: *"Yes, people often see starting a new job as a way of turning the page on their careers."*
> 
> **Feifei**: *"对，人们常把**换新工作**视为职业生涯的翻页。"*

| 表达 | 侧重 | 典型语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **turn over a new leaf** | 改变行为/习惯 | 戒坏习惯、健身、学习 |
| **turn the page** | 结束阶段/翻篇 | 换工作、分手、搬家、毕业 |

## 批判性思考题

1. **隐喻的认知力量**：*leaf = page* 将抽象"改变"具象化为"翻书页"。这种 *conceptual metaphor* 如何降低行为改变的心理门槛？(*embodied cognition, spatial metaphor for time*)

2. **新年决心的失败率**：研究显示 80% 的 *new year's resolutions* 2 月前失败。如何用英语表达 *"implementation intention" (实施意图)*、* "habit stacking" (习惯叠加)*、* "identity-based habits" (身份认同习惯)* 来提高成功率？

3. **Turn over a new leaf vs Turn the page 的细微差别**：前者强调 *agency* (主动性/行为改变)，后者强调 *closure* (了结/阶段结束)。心理学上对应 *"behavioral activation"* vs *"narrative closure"*。

4. **文化差异中的"重新开始"**：中文"洗心革面""痛改前非""重新做人""开启新篇章" 对应不同程度的改变。日本 *"kaizen" (改善)*、韩国 *"새출발" (新出发)* 如何对应？*cultural scripts for redemption*

5. **系统性改变 vs 个人决心**：*turn over a new leaf* 暗示个人意志力。结构性因素（环境、社会支持、政策）如何用英语纳入？(*structural determinants, enabling environment, nudge theory*)

## 完整对话记录

### Transcript

**Feifei**: Hello, and welcome to The English We Speak, where we explain phrases used by fluent English speakers so that you can use them too. I'm Feifei, and I'm with Phil.

**Phil**: Hi, any plans for 2025, Feifei?

**Feifei**: It's a new year so I think it's time to **turn over a new leaf**.

**Phil**: **Turn over a new leaf**? I'm not sure it's the right time of year to be doing gardening, Feifei.

**Feifei**: It's not that kind of **leaf**, and it's just an expression. It means to make a change and make a new start. You stop doing something negative. '**Turn over a new leaf**' is the expression we're learning in this programme.

**Phil**: So, it's a bit like making a **new year's resolution**?

**Feifei**: Yes. The '**leaf**' in this expression is a **leaf of paper**, like a sheet of paper, so to '**turn over a new leaf**' means to turn the page and start a new chapter of your story. You're trying to move on from something negative. For example, I've been feeling very **inactive**. I've never managed to get into going to the gym, but in 2025 I'm going to **turn over a new leaf** and get fit. Phil, what about you? Do you think you should **turn over a new leaf**?

**Phil**: **Turn over a new leaf** for 2025? Hmm, maybe I should start eating more healthily. I love cooking, but nothing I cook is that healthy, and I love to give myself huge portions. That's probably something negative that I should change. Yes, that's how I'm going to **turn over a new leaf**. Now let's hear how some of our colleagues are going to **turn over a new leaf**.

**Examples**:
> I've got this terrible habit of biting my nails, but I've decided I need to **turn over a new leaf**, so in the new year I'm stopping that. That's my **new year's resolution**.

> I was a bit naughty in primary school sometimes, but my teacher told me to **turn over a new leaf**, and I did. I was very **well-behaved** from then on.

> So, these days I always look at my phone instead of trying to remember things that I probably know, and so I'm going to **turn over a new leaf** in the new year and use my brain instead and try to remember things.

**Feifei**: Of course, it's not just about **new year's resolutions**. Any time you want to put something negative behind you and make a change, you can try to **turn over a new leaf**.

**Phil**: A similar expression that people use is '**turn the page**'. We can use it metaphorically to talk about starting a new chapter in our lives.

**Feifei**: Yes, people often see starting a new job as a way of **turning the page** on their careers.

**Phil**: We'll finish here, but if you feel like you don't know enough expressions and idioms in English and you want to **turn over a new leaf**, come back for the next episode, see you then!

**Feifei**: Bye!
